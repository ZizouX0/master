# Wave 2 — Erasmus Mundus funding (F-EM), verified 2026-08-22

- **Catalogue swept:** the official EACEA EMJM catalogue, all 11 pages, **212 distinct programmes** (220 project entries). Filtered against the client's ten paths; **24 EMJMs + 1 non-EMJM (EIT Digital) logged** in `funding_erasmus_mundus.csv` (`FEM-001` is a scheme-level baseline row, `FEM-002`…`FEM-025` are programmes).
- **With a Spain / Netherlands / Berlin partner: 9** — EMAI (Barcelona **and** Nijmegen), GLOCAL (Barcelona **and** Rotterdam), MULTICOM (Murcia), IPCVai (Madrid), EDISS (Palma), MAGMa (Rotterdam), Mundus Journalism (Amsterdam), Euroculture (Groningen, coordinator), CLMCE (Tilburg), plus BDMA/WAVES which are now dead. **No EMJM with a Berlin partner was found.** Berlin is essentially absent from the EMJM network.
- **Current scholarship value (2021-27 rules, read from the Programme Guide):** flat **€1,400/month × 24 months = €33,600**, covering *travel, visa, installation and subsistence together* — there is **no separate travel/installation top-up and no higher Partner-Country rate any more**. On top, "projects cannot charge tuition fees or other mandatory costs … to Erasmus Mundus scholarship holders", so total value runs **€50k–€82k** depending on the programme's fee level.
- **Tunisia is not just eligible — it is a named EU budget target:** *"Region 3 (Neighbourhood South): 18% of the budget available from NDICI. At least 20% of this amount should be allocated to Tunisia."* Region-3 top-up scholarships are exempt from the 10%-per-nationality cap. **No 12-month residence rule exists in the current EMJM guide** (it belonged to the pre-2021 scheme).
- **300-ECTS engineering diploma:** clears every EMJM bar found (all ask 180 ECTS / EQF 6). Credit volume is never the obstacle — **discipline and portfolio are**.

1. **DIGICREA** (FEM-023) — A|G|C|N|H. Dedicated **Sound-Music specialisation**, signal processing + acoustics + sound design + XR + creative coding. €33,600 and **zero tuition even self-funded**. Saint-Étienne / Porto / Katowice — no target node, logged as outstanding-fit exception. Call for 2027-2029 **opens November 2026**. Portfolio: 3–5 sound-music works.
2. **ReSound** (FEM-002) — G|A|H|N. The only pure **Sound Design for Media Arts** EMJM. IADT Dublin / Lusófona Lisbon / LMTA Vilnius. €33,600 + €15,000 fee waiver. Portfolio gate; 2027 dates unpublished.
3. **EMAI** (FEM-008) — C|A|N. **UPF Barcelona (coordinator) + Radboud Nijmegen** — the only programme hitting both target countries with a technical curriculum, and UPF hosts the Music Technology Group. Earliest deadline pattern (~20 Dec). ⚠ upf.edu was Cloudflare-blocked all session — **re-verify first**.
4. **EDISS** (FEM-013) — C|N. Åbo Akademi + **Universitat de les Illes Balears (Palma)**. Perfect academic match for a software engineer; three separate funding rounds. Scholarship round closed 8 Jan in the 2026 cycle.
5. **GLOCAL** (FEM-009) — L|J|AC|AD. **Universitat de Barcelona + Erasmus University Rotterdam**. The best-funded route for the business/management paths (self-funded would cost €48,720, so the waiver is worth ~€82k).

## Coverage confidence — honest
**Good (≈85%) on breadth**, weaker on depth. The full 212-programme catalogue was enumerated and read, so nothing on-catalogue was missed by name. Limits: (a) the catalogue's country/university facets are POST-only and could not be queried, so partner countries were confirmed programme-by-programme and only for the ~25 shortlisted — a relevant Spain/NL partner may sit inside a programme I triaged out by title; (b) **upf.edu, globallocal-erasmusmundus.eu and deai.ulb.be were unreachable** (Cloudflare/503), leaving EMAI, GLOCAL and DEAI partially or un-verified; (c) an aggregator claims the Commission announced **37 newly selected EMJM projects on 16 July 2026** that will recruit in autumn 2026 for a **September 2027** start — **the EACEA catalogue only lists selection years 2017-2025 and I could not confirm this on any official page.** If true, the 2027-intake field is materially larger than this file and the catalogue must be re-swept in **October 2026**.
