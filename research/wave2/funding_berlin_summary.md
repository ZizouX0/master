# Wave 2 — Berlin funding summary (agent F-BE, verified 2026-08-22)

- **29 schemes logged** (`FBE-001`…`FBE-029`); 21 VERIFIED, 8 PARTIALLY_VERIFIED. **14 Tunisia-eligible `yes`, 5 `likely`, 2 `unclear`, 8 `no`.**
- **Best 3 realistic routes.** (1) **Avicenna-Studienwerk ISP** — 844 €/mo + 100 € insurance + 100 € mobility = **1,044 €/mo**, all subjects, explicitly for non-*Bildungsinländer*, no German condition, window ~1 Mar–15 Apr 2027. (2) **Erasmus Mundus Joint Master** — the only award covering tuition + travel + **visa** + living allowance; apply **Oct 2026–Jan 2027** for Sept 2027. (3) **Heinrich-Böll Studienwerk international track** — explicit *priority for DAC-country applicants still at home* (Tunisia qualifies), deadline 1 March 2027; amount TBC. Runners-up: **DAAD EPOS** 992 €/mo (blocked by its 2-years-work-experience bar) and **Rosa-Luxemburg** 992 €/mo (blocked by proven B2 German).
- **True cost of Berlin.** No tuition at state universities for bachelor's and **consecutive** master's (FU: "erhebt – außer für weiterbildende Studienangebote – keine Studiengebühren"); *weiterbildende*/executive master's DO charge. Semester fee WS 2026/27: **TU 379.06 € · HWR 379.77 € · FU 376.80 € · BHT 376.30 € · HTW 375.30 €** ≈ **750–760 €/year**, and 226.80 € of that buys the Germany-wide *Deutschlandsemesterticket*. HU and UdK figures = TBC.
- **Blocked account: 11,904 € (992 €/month)**, German Embassy Tunis *Check-list Études*; since Feb 2023 only a **German** Sperrkonto is accepted (Tunisian ones no longer are). National visa fee 75 €.
- **A scholarship removes the blocked account entirely.** The Embassy's separate *Boursiers d'État allemand/Erasmus+* checklist asks only for the award letter, university confirmation and insurance — no proof of funds — plus direct TLScontact booking (no waiting list) and a waived visa fee. Winning a public scholarship is therefore worth ~12,000 € of liquidity on top of the stipend.
- **German is a hard funding condition** at: FES (application in German, "very good command"), Friedrich-Naumann (documents *and* interview in German even for English-taught programmes), Rosa-Luxemburg (B2 proven), Hanns-Seidel (B2 proven, no exceptions), KAS ("gute Deutschkenntnisse"), Avicenna *regular* track (C1/DSH-2), DAAD EPOS German-track (B1 → DSH-2/TestDaF-4). Helmut-Schmidt imposes a **compulsory 4-month German course** as a condition of the award. **No German condition:** Avicenna ISP, Deutschlandstipendium, Erasmus Mundus, Catalyst Berlin, and the DAAD artist lines (language of instruction only).
- **Earliest deadlines.** Böll round now open, closes **1 Sep 2026**; **DAAD Study Scholarship Music 24 Sep 2026** (selection Feb 2027, funding from Oct 2027); RLS 1 Oct 2026; **DAAD Fine Art/Design/Film and Performing Arts 15 Oct 2026**; FNS 31 Oct 2026; EMJM calls open Oct 2026. **Already missed: Helmut-Schmidt-Programme, closed 31 July 2026** (and no Berlin institution participates anyway).
- **Doors that are shut** (logged with evidence): DAAD Study Scholarships all disciplines and **DAAD STEM scholarships** (Tunisia absent from both country lists — the STEM exclusion is the most painful, it was the perfect subject fit); Studienstiftung and Avicenna's regular track (§8 BAföG); Hans-Böckler (internationals = PhD only); Cusanuswerk (Catholic membership); Elsa-Neumann (doctorate only); **and there is no Tunisia- or Maghreb-specific DAAD master's line at all** — the Tunis office runs only research/PhD/training calls.
- **Coverage confidence: high** on DAAD, the eleven Begabtenförderungswerke, Berlin public-university costs and the visa figures — all read on the providers' own domains, several in German or French. **Medium-low** on Berlin institution-level pools and private schools: HU Berlin sits behind an anti-bot wall, and SRH, Macromedia, BIMM, dBs and ESMT publish fees only through JavaScript-rendered pages that could not be read. Named gaps: Böll's amount for internationals, the EMJM monthly allowance, HSS details, TU/FU/HU/BHT STIBET equivalents, HU/UdK semester fees.
