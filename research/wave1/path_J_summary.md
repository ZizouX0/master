# Path J — Music Business / Management — Wave-1 summary (W1-J, 2026-08-22)

**Rows: 19** — Spain 8, Netherlands 5 (incl. 1 Erasmus Mundus with a Dutch partner), Berlin 4, plus 1 flagged exception (Popakademie Mannheim, `in_scope = flagged_exception`). Verification: 14 VERIFIED, 4 PARTIALLY_VERIFIED, 1 UNVERIFIED (UNIR — unir.net returns HTTP 403 to all automated access; every field left TBC).

**Real accredited degrees vs private/own-title awards.** Only **7 of 19** are official state-recognised degrees: Berklee Valencia GEMB (Spanish *título oficial* accredited with UPV **and** US NECHE), the four Dutch NVAO research-university MAs (EUR ×2, RUG, UU), FU Berlin (public German M.A.), Macromedia Berlin (private but *staatlich anerkannt*), and Popakademie (public, ACQUIN). Spain's music-business market is dominated by ***títulos propios*** — ESMUC, UC3M MiMeS, UB-IL3, UAM, UAX and SAE/UDIMA are all own-title awards, which weakens scholarship access and the Tunisian student visa. Two Berlin rows (BIMM, Catalyst) award **UK** degrees (BIMM University; Plymouth Marjon) taught in Berlin — not German degrees; this must be cleared with the Ausländerbehörde before ranking them.

**Coverage confidence.** Spain **high** (national landscape mapped; the only real hole is UNIR, blocked at source). Berlin **high–medium** (BIMM is the only dedicated music-business master in the city; SRH Berlin School of Popular Arts was checked and has *no* music-business master, only Classical Music / World Music / Film / Photography / design MAs; hdpk.de and Deutsche POP could not be reached). Netherlands **medium** — and the key finding is negative: **no Dutch institution offers a dedicated music-business master**. HKU's Music Management is a *bachelor* specialisation only; Fontys Rockacademie's Business Manager exit profile is a bachelor; Herman Brood is MBO; SAE Amsterdam's MA has been withdrawn (page 404s). The Dutch options are economics/cultural-studies adjacencies.

**Strongest three finds.** (1) **Berklee Valencia — MA Global Entertainment and Music Business**: official Spanish + US degree, English, 12 months, no music degree required, portfolio is a 1-minute *business-idea video* not an audition — but USD 50,430 with scholarships capped at 90%. (2) **Macromedia Berlin — M.A. Music Management (EN)**: English-taught, state-recognised, no subject bar, and the client's **300 ECTS clears the 210-ECTS threshold → the shorter 3-semester/90-ECTS route**, saving ~€6,840 and six months. (3) **MAGMa Erasmus Mundus** (EUR Rotterdam partner): the only **full+stipend** funding on this path (€33,600), explicitly open to Tunisian nationals — at the cost of being art/heritage rather than music.

**Best `accepts_engineering_bachelor` signals:** UB-IL3 lists *enginyeria* explicitly; UAM lists *Ingeniería*; SAE/UDIMA lists *Ingeniería*; ESMUC accepts "any university degree with demonstrated musical skills". UC3M names Sound Engineering among recommended backgrounds.

**Gaps / Wave-2 actions.** (a) UNIR must be checked in a real browser — it may be Spain's only *official* music-business master. (b) UAX, SAE Spain and Popakademie publish **no tuition figure**; Berklee publishes only USD. (c) No 2027-cycle deadline is confirmed anywhere except **RUG (1 May 2027 for 1 Sep 2027)**; UC3M's 2026/27 round is already closed. (d) hdpk Berlin, Deutsche POP, Microfusa, Taller de Músics, Nebrija, UCJC and Universidad Europea could not be confirmed (bot-blocked or JS-rendered listings). (e) Tunisia eligibility for Erasmus Trustfonds (EUR) and Macromedia's partial scholarships is unresolved. (f) Two schedule/visa questions: UB-IL3 is evenings-only and HKU Crossover Creativity is Dutch-taught part-time — neither may support a study residence permit.
