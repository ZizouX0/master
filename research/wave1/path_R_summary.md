# Path R — Live Sound & Event Systems Engineering — Wave 1 summary (W1-R, 2026-08-22)

**14 rows.** Award-level split: **6 master's-level** (R-001 BHT, R-002 TU Berlin, R-003 UPV, R-004 UPM, R-014 WAVES = real accredited degrees; R-005 SAE Spain = *título propio*, R-006 SAE Amsterdam = UK-awarded, no NVAO found) · **1 postgraduate diploma** (R-009) · **2 private certificates** (R-007, R-008) · **3 vocational** (R-010, R-011, R-012) · **1 bachelor logged deliberately** (R-013) to record the Dutch ceiling.

**Coverage confidence.** Berlin: **high** — BHT and TU Berlin are the only two Berlin master's that touch this field and both were verified on their own domains. Spain: **medium-high** — all four named official acoustics masters plus every private academy on the floor list were checked; UPM's own programme site is stale (2024/25) and several private prices are simply unpublished. Netherlands: **high confidence in a negative result** — checked AHK/ATD, SAE Amsterdam, Herman Brood, and the MBO landscape.

**Strongest finds.** (1) **BHT Berlin, Veranstaltungstechnik und -management M.Eng.** — a genuine accredited, tuition-free engineering master's in event/theatre technology; the only one of its kind in scope. Two blockers: German-taught, and **summer-semester-only intake**, so a September 2027 start is impossible. (2) **TU Berlin, Audiokommunikation und -technologie M.Sc.** — tuition-free, winter intake, admission text explicitly names engineering degrees and requires exactly the maths + programming the client has (`yes_explicit`); gate is German C1. (3) **UPV Gandia, Máster en Ingeniería Acústica** — *título oficial*, €4,240/yr non-EU, with a non-EU application phase opening in November. (4) **WAVES EMJM** (UPV partner, English, funded) — but **recruitment is suspended**; re-check before the 2027 cycle.

**Where the field has no master's-level provision.** The **Netherlands has none at all**: AHK's Design & Technology (sound design / technical production) is the country's only HBO-level programme and stops at bachelor; AHK's masters sit in DAS Graduate School and are artistic-research, not technical. Everything else Dutch — Herman Brood, Deltion, ROC Midden Nederland, ROC van Amsterdam, Albeda, Noorderpoort, SintLucas — is MBO niveau 4, i.e. secondary vocational, far below the client's 300 ECTS. **Spain has no master's in live sound either**: the subject is taught at CFGS/FP level (ESTAE, CEV) or as private non-degree courses (Microfusa 11 weeks; Trade Formación 500 h — the most on-target *content* found anywhere, and worth nothing as a credential). Spain's only degree-level route is acoustics engineering.

**Accreditation / visa flags.** SAE Spain masters are *títulos propios* via UDIMA, **not** in RUCT. SAE Amsterdam's master is awarded by the University of Hertfordshire and SAE's own Dutch accreditation page makes **no NVAO claim** — a likely blocker for an IND study permit; its course page 404s. Trade Formación and Microfusa are private certificates that cannot support a student visa. BHT and TU Berlin are *staatlich anerkannt* and clean.

**Not logged, deliberately** (checked and rejected as off-path or out of scope): SAE Germany's M.A. Professional Media Creation (Folkwang-awarded, real degree, but **Bochum only** and music-production focused); Catalyst Berlin MA Creative Production (Music) and BIMM Berlin MA (music production, another path); BUas MSc Leisure & Tourism Studies (leisure management, not event systems); ESMUC Sonologia (bachelor specialisation). One unverified lead left open: Universidad San Jorge *Experto en Luz y Sonido* — only aggregator sources reachable before the search budget ran out.
