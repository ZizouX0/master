# Path H — Immersive / Spatial Audio — Wave 1 summary (W1-H, 2026-08-22)

**Rows: 17** (H-001…H-017). Full degrees: **17** — but only **5** are genuinely spatial-audio-led (H-001 module-level, H-002, H-003, H-004, H-005); the other 12 are tracks/labs/components inside a broader master, or logged negatives. No standalone "MSc Spatial Audio" exists in Spain, NL or Berlin — as expected for a specialist field.

**Coverage confidence.** Berlin: **high** — TU Berlin, UdK and Catalyst (ex-dBs) all verified on official pages; SAE Berlin and HTW Berlin not reached. Netherlands: **high** for The Hague/Utrecht/Amsterdam (Sonology, HKU, SAE all opened); **medium overall** — Conservatorium van Amsterdam, ArtEZ, TU Delft, TU Eindhoven and 4DSOUND unchecked. Spain: **medium-high** — Barcelona, Madrid, Valencia and Zaragoza covered; UPF unreachable, and Málaga / Illes Balears / Carlos III not reached.

**Strongest three finds.** (1) **H-004 TU Berlin + Institute of Sonology double degree** — the two leading wave-field-synthesis institutions on the path, Berlin+NL in one programme, TU Berlin year free; blocked by an *unresolved conflict* between the two partners' official pages over whether German TestDaF TDN 4 is required. (2) **H-002 Master of Music in Sonology, The Hague** — dedicated WFS studio, "composing with Wave Field Synthesis" taught, computer science and electronic engineering named as accepted backgrounds, NL Scholarship open to non-EEA; €11,488/yr non-EEA and portfolio-gated. (3) **H-001 La Salle URL Barcelona** — the only *official* Spanish master's with a named immersive-audio module ("Sound recording and immersive audio", 5 ECTS) plus 3D audio and auralization, English-taught, 10 months.

**Runner-up worth ranking:** H-005 Catalyst Berlin MA — English-taught in Berlin, Dolby Atmos 7.1.4 studio, spatial-sound workshops, Monom access, €11,693/yr, UK-awarded degree.

**Gaps / must-do in Wave 2.** UPF Barcelona is a **DEAD_LINK** (Cloudflare blocked every attempt) — re-verify from another path. Tuition is **TBC** for La Salle, HKU non-EEA, ENTI-UB, UB Art Sonor, CSMV, SONTIC and the SAE Amsterdam MA. Unchecked institutions: Conservatorium van Amsterdam, ArtEZ, TU Delft, TU Eindhoven, University of Twente, Willem de Kooning, SAE Berlin/Barcelona/Madrid, HTW Berlin, Hanns Eisler Berlin, U. Málaga, UIB, Carlos III. The web-search budget was exhausted mid-sweep, so Spanish/Dutch/German-language discovery searches ("audio inmersivo", "ruimtelijk geluid", "Raumklang") were not completed.

**Negatives worth keeping.** H-016 UdK *Sound Studies and Sonic Arts* is **closed to new students** (ZIW page verbatim) while its English course pages still advertise applications — a trap for later waves. H-017 **Erasmus Mundus WAVES** suspended recruitment as of 1 Oct 2025 with no 2026-2028 cohort; re-check for a 2027-2029 call. H-009 UPM Acoustics has **zero** spatial content in its verified subject list.

**Client-specific flags.** Portfolio required at H-002, H-005, H-006, H-012, H-013 — flagged, not excluded. German C1 blocks H-003 and probably H-004. The 300-ECTS engineering diploma is explicitly sufficient at H-002, H-003, H-004 and H-010; it is *weakest* at H-013 (ESMUC demands a higher degree in music). Award-type red flags: título propio at H-010, H-011, H-013, H-014; UK-awarded (non-NVAO / non-staatlich) at H-005 and H-015.
