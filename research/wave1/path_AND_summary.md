# Wave 1 gap-fill — ANDALUSIA (W1-AND) — 39 rows, verified 2026-08-22

**Central question answered: Andalusia applies NO nationality-based tuition surcharge.**
Per **Decreto 98/2023 (BOJA 84, 05/05/2023), Anexo I apdo. 1**, whose only column heading is
*"Precio según la vez de matrícula — 1.ª 2.ª 3.ª 4.ª y ss."* — bands are keyed **solely to enrolment attempt and
experimentality group; no column is keyed to nationality or residence**. **Artículo 3** ("Precios para el estudiantado
no nacional de un Estado miembro del EEE o de Suiza") states non-EEA students pay *"los precios previstos en este
decreto"*; art. 3.2 merely **permits** a university to vary the credit price for **non-residents** (cap: 100% of cost of
service). **Decreto 142/2025 (BOJA 172, 08/09/2025)** amends only arts. 5/6/12/14/DF1 and Anexo I *apdo. 2*
(administrative fees) — it leaves art. 3 and the per-credit table **untouched**, so these rates stand for 2026/27.

**Reconciling €13.68 vs €27.60:** not a contradiction and neither is a non-EU rate. **€13.68** = 1st-enrolment,
non-*habilitante* master (all groups). **€12.62** = 1st-enrolment *habilitante* master (regulated professions, e.g.
Ing. de Telecomunicación). **€27.35** = 2nd enrolment. **€27.60 is obsolete** — it appears only on UGR's acoustics
programme page, which still cites the superseded **Decreto 329/2010**; UGR's own Escuela de Posgrado fee page (opened
today) gives €13.68. Flagged as a CONFLICT on AND-002.
