# TIMELINE — September 2026 → September 2027
**Built 2026-08-22. Worked backward from the earliest verified deadline.**

Only **verified or partially-verified** dates appear here. Anything unverified is in GAPS.md and
deliberately absent from this calendar — a date that might be wrong is worse than a blank.

## The three dates that set everything else

| Date | What | Why it dominates |
|---|---|---|
| **1 Dec 2026** | TU Delft **van Effen** scholarship closes | Full tuition **+ living costs**, open to Tunisians — and it closes **six weeks before** the programme's own deadline. Miss it and Delft becomes a €25,633/yr proposition. |
| **12 Jan 2027, 12:00 CET** | Netherlands Film Academy | The only hard, timed, 2027-dated deadline in the sweep. |
| **29 Jan 2027** | Andalusian **DUA Fase 1** | A round reserved exclusively for foreign-qualification applicants, covering nine universities on one form. Three of your seven applications ride on this single date. |

**The structural trap, stated once:** Dutch and German scholarship deadlines fall *before* the
programme deadlines they fund. Applying "on time" for the programme routinely means applying too
late for the money. Every scholarship date below is therefore listed as its own line.

## Month by month

### Sept 2026 — the language decision, and the venture
- **Decide on German, now.** TU Berlin's Audiokommunikation is the best subject fit in Europe and its
  only gate is C1. From a standing start that is 12–18 months. Deciding in January is deciding no.
- **Register the venture.** ESMT's Panzer Scholarship (€35,000) requires a founded business. It is the
  one eligibility condition on these 197 rows you can create rather than satisfy.
- **Book the English test.** IELTS Academic. Required scores range from B1 (UJA Linares) to
  **IELTS 6.5 no band below 6.0** (EIT Digital) to **TOEFL 100** (Berklee). Book for **Nov–Dec**:
  results take ~13 days, and reporting to institutions takes longer.
- Start the **degree translation + apostille** chain from Tunisia. This is the long pole and it is
  invisible until it blocks you.
- **1 Sept 2026** — Heinrich-Böll Studienwerk round opens.

### Oct 2026
- **Erasmus Mundus calls open** (Oct–Nov). Re-sweep the EACEA catalogue: an unconfirmed report says
  37 new EMJM projects were selected in July 2026 for Sept 2027 starts. If true, the field is larger
  than anything in this dataset.
- **1 Oct** — Rosa-Luxemburg deadline (requires B2 German).
- **15 Oct** — DAAD Fine Art / Design / Film and Performing Arts lines close. *Noted for completeness:
  these are open to Tunisians but require a first degree in music or the arts, which you do not have.*
- Draft the statement of purpose once, in a form that forks cleanly for a technical audience
  (TU Berlin, EIT) and a business one (ESMT, Macromedia).

### Nov 2026
- **1 Nov** — Berklee Valencia Early Action application.
- **15 Nov** — Berklee **scholarship** deadline. Two weeks after the application; both or neither.
- **1 Nov** — UMA document deadline (its art. 3.2 residence clause is a paperwork risk, not a fee one).
- **Sit the English test.**

### Dec 2026 — the crunch
- **1 Dec** — **TU Delft van Effen.** Highest-value single deadline of the year.
- **~20 Dec** — EMAI (UPF Barcelona + Radboud) closes, on the 2026 comparator.
- Erasmus Mundus closings cluster from here into January.
- **EIT Digital Period 1 must be submitted.** Period 2 is not an option for an applicant in Tunisia.

### Jan 2027 — the heaviest month
- **8 Jan** — EDISS (2026 comparator).
- **12 Jan, 12:00 CET** — **Netherlands Film Academy.** Hard cutoff, results mid-April.
- **13–29 Jan** — **Andalusian DUA Fase 1**, foreign-qualification round. One form, six ranked
  choices, covering UMA, UGR, UJA and the rest. **No homologación required.**
- **19 Jan** — MAGMa (2026 comparator).
- Mid-Jan — most Dutch non-EEA scholarship deadlines.
- **15 Feb** — TU Berlin / Sonology double degree.

### Feb–Mar 2027
- **19 Feb, 12 Mar** — DUA Fase 1 adjudications.
- **1 Mar** — **Heinrich-Böll international track.** Explicit priority for DAC-country applicants
  still resident in their home country: you qualify structurally, not just nominally.
- **1 Mar – 15 Apr** — **Avicenna-Studienwerk ISP.** €1,044/month, all subjects, **no German
  requirement** — the rarest combination among German foundations.

### Apr–May 2027
- **1 Apr** — Radboud non-EU deadline. *(Wave 1 had this as 1 July. It is not.)*
- **9 Apr** — final DUA Fase 1 adjudication.
- **15 Apr** — **Erasmus Rotterdam non-EEA.** *(Not 1 May — that is the English-proof deadline.)*
- **1 May** — RUG deadline for a 1 Sept 2027 start.
- **15 May** — TU Berlin IMES.
- **15 Jun** — TU Berlin Audiokommunikation. The latest meaningful deadline in the sweep.

### Jun–Sept 2027 — visa, and the thing that actually stops people
- **17–26 Jun** — DUA Fase 2 (ordinaria), if Fase 1 missed.
- **Germany:** blocked account of **€11,904**, and since Feb 2023 only a German *Sperrkonto* counts.
  **A scholarship removes this entirely** — the Embassy in Tunis runs a separate checklist for
  scholarship holders requiring no proof of funds, skipping the waiting list, and waiving the €75 fee.
  This is the strongest argument for chasing funding even where tuition is already zero.
- **Spain / Netherlands:** student visa via the consulate; budget 8–12 weeks and expect the
  apostilled translations from September 2026 to be requested again.

## Portfolio gates, and when they bite

Only three shortlisted rows are portfolio-gated, and none needs finished music:
- **Netherlands Film Academy** — by 12 Jan 2027. The binding one.
- **UdK Design & Computation** — an unusually open format, not a showreel.
- **Berklee GEMB** — a **one-minute self-produced video pitching a business idea**. Not an audition.

Your portfolio being in progress costs you far less than it appears. The programmes that would have
demanded a finished body of work — UdK Tonmeister, the conservatoire routes — are closed to you on
credential grounds anyway, and Tonmeister additionally requires an audiogram proving hearing across
125 Hz–8 kHz. Build the portfolio for your career, not for these applications.
