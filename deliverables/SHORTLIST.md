## TOP 3 OVERALL

### 1. TU Berlin — Audiokommunikation und -technologie, M.Sc. `A,C,G,H,R`
**€0 tuition · 120 ECTS · 24 months · Berlin · VERIFIED**
The best credential fit found anywhere: the admission text names engineering degrees first and asks
for exactly the maths and programming you already hold — the only `yes_explicit` on its path, from a
group that is a European leader in spatial audio and wave field synthesis.
Cost is a €380 semester fee, ~€750/year, including a Germany-wide transport ticket.
**The whole decision is German.** Instruction is German with English electives; you need C1 plus
English B2. Nothing else blocks you.
**Deadline:** 15 June (window 1 Apr–15 Jun, 2026 cycle — 2027 TBC). Unusually late; it does not
compete with the December scramble.
**Biggest risk:** you cannot reach C1 by mid-2027 from a standing start alongside final-year study.

### 2. Universidad de Málaga — Máster en Ingeniería Acústica `A,H,R`
**€820.80 total · 60 ECTS · 12 months · Málaga · VERIFIED**
A real *título oficial* in acoustics for the price of a phone, inside the telecommunications school,
with an anechoic Audio & Electroacoustics Lab. Its access list names *Ingeniería Informática*
explicitly. Andalusia levies no nationality surcharge — confirmed against Decreto 98/2023 — and at
UMA a student-stay authorisation counts as residence, the opposite of Madrid.
**Deadline:** 29 January, in a Fase 1 round reserved exclusively for foreign-qualification applicants.
**Biggest risk:** Spanish B2, and acoustics is engineering, not music-making.

### 3. EIT Digital Master School — HCID/XR `N`
**€18,000/yr list, but full tuition waiver open to all nationalities · 120 ECTS · Twente + Madrid · VERIFIED**
The correction that rescued this one: Wave 1 recorded EIT funding as closed to non-EU applicants.
It is not. The full and half tuition waivers state *"Available to: All students"* — only the
Scholarship of Excellence, which adds a living allowance, is EU-only. A BME waiver stacks on top,
and an EIF plan defers all fees until six months after graduation.
**Deadline:** 2027 dates unpublished; the 2026 equivalent closed in early January.
**Biggest risk:** you must apply in **Period 1**. In Period 2, Twente and UNITN admit non-EU entrants
only if they already hold a Dutch permit — applying from Tunisia in Period 2 is an automatic reject.

## THE ONE TO ACT ON FIRST — and it is not on the list above

**ESMT Berlin, Panzer Scholarship: €35,000 against €36,000 of tuition**, plus a linked award covering
ten months of Berlin housing. Eligibility: **under 30, a citizen and resident of an African country,
and has founded a business or social enterprise.**

You satisfy two of three conditions today. The third is the only requirement in this entire dataset
that you can *manufacture*: register a small venture around your production or music-tech work, now,
so the condition is satisfied by the 2027 application. Nothing else on these 197 rows converts
effort into €35,000 so directly.
