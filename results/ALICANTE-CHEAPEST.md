# Cheapest English-taught master's in or near Alicante — with a visa letter

**Checked 26 September 2026.** Prices are for **2026-27**; 2027-28 prices are not published yet.
Distances are by road from Alicante city.

> **Short answer:** there is **no private English-taught in-person master's in Alicante province
> itself**. Universidad Europea's Alicante campus, CEU's Elche campus and Fundesem only teach in
> Spanish. The cheapest private option is **UCAM in Murcia (80 km)**. The cheapest option of all is
> **public: Universidad de Alicante / UMH at about €4,240 a year** — but their admission dates are
> late for a visa.

---

## 1. Private — cheapest first

| Hospitality Management | UCAM, Murcia | Official | €7,600 | €1,630 | same |
| Global MBA+ | UCV, Valencia (175 km) | Official | €7,500 | **50 % = €3,750** | ❌ only if the delay "is not attributable" to you |
| Marketing & Communication | UCAM, Murcia | Official | €7,800 | €1,630 | same as UCAM |
| MBA | UCAM, Murcia | Official | €10,200 | €1,630 | same |
| Digital Marketing · Project Mgmt & AI · Intl Relations · Geopolitics | Universidad Europea, Valencia (175 km) | Mostly *título propio* | €11,200 – €11,400 | €1,200 reservation (+ an "enrolment" payment of unclear amount) | ✅ reservation refunded with the embassy's refusal letter |
| International Trade | ENAE, Murcia (80 km) | Official (Univ. of Murcia) | €12,500 | €300 booking fee | not published; ⚠️ classes only Fri afternoon + Sat morning |

**UCAM extra fees:** add **€489** to every UCAM price (€150 application + €280 pre-inscription +
€59.12 insurance). So the cheapest real total is **€5,989**.

⚠️ **Confirm with UCAM before paying:** an old (2020) unofficial agent guide says UCAM asked
international students for **50 % of tuition** as the reservation before the visa letter. UCAM's
own 2026 page says €1,200. Ask them which applies to you.

**UCAM refund wording:** *"A refund of the reservation fee will be issued if an applicant is unable
to take their place due to a student visa denial (administrative fees are non-refundable)"* — the
visa application must be made before the start date, with proof of refusal.

## 2. Public — cheaper, and in Alicante itself

In the Valencia region, non-EU students with a student visa pay **double** the normal price:
**€70.68 per ECTS → €4,240 for 60 ECTS** (Decreto 101/2024, art. 6). Add about €190 in admin fees
and **€155.22** to check a non-European degree.

| Programme | University | Language | Tuition (non-EU) | Deposit | Enrolment date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Economics with Data Science** | **Universidad de Alicante** (San Vicente campus) | Fully English, in person, 25 places | **~€4,240** | €100 (not refunded if you don't enrol) | late July |
| **Neuroscience: from Research to the Clinic** | **UMH** (Sant Joan d'Alacant, 8 km) | Fully English, in person | ~€4,240 | €150 | mid July |
| Artificial Intelligence | Universidad de Alicante | Fully English, **blended** | ~€4,240 | €100 | late July |
| Nanoscience & Molecular Nanotechnology / Advanced Materials | UA (joint masters) | English | ~€4,240 | €100 | late July — only 10 / 4 places |

**The catch — timing:** UA and UMH only publish admissions in **July** and enrol at the end of
July. That leaves very little time to get a visa for a September/October start. If you go public,
apply in the **first window (mid-March 2027)**. Murcia's public university admits in **May**, but it
has no fully English in-person master's at the public price.

---

## 3. Recommendation

1. **Cheapest private and visa-safe:** **UCAM Murcia** — Bilingual Education or International
   Relations at **€5,500 (+€489)**. You risk **€430** if the visa is refused. Murcia is 1 hour from
   Alicante by train or bus.
2. **Cheapest overall, in Alicante:** **Universidad de Alicante — Economics with Data Science**
   (~€4,430 all-in). Apply in March, and ask UA early whether they can issue an admission letter in
   time for the visa.
3. **If you want a refund-safe deposit and don't mind Valencia:** Universidad Europea de Valencia
   (€11,200+).
