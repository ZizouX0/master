## Best plan

1. **Now:** get your degree and transcript **apostilled and translated**.
2. **Early October, apply to 2–3 at once:**
   - **UPC** if your bachelor's is engineering, science, maths or computing — cheapest by far
     (€330). Structural & Construction opens **7 Oct**, MAMME **16 Oct**; email the EETAC / ETSETB
     schools for an early decision.
   - **UCAM International Relations** (any degree) — `admissions@ucam.edu`.
   - **La Salle International MBA** (any degree, €1,000, starts 1 March).
3. Write in every application that you need the letter **by 30 November 2026** for your visa, and
   pay as soon as you're admitted.
