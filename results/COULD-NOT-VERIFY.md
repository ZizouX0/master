## 2. Systematic gaps that apply broadly (by design, given the July-2026 timing)
- **2027 application dates are mostly not published yet.** Anything marked `PRIOR CYCLE — confirm new date` shows the *last* cycle's dates as a guide. The 2027 windows will appear on the official pages between **~Oct 2026 and Feb 2027**. A handful are already confirmed as real future dates (Chevening 6 Oct 2026; KTH opens 16 Oct 2026 / closes 15 Jan 2027; Türkiye Bursları 10 Jan–20 Feb annually).
- **Exact international tuition** is sometimes only available via a university "fee calculator" rather than a published figure (e.g. HKU Utrecht). Marked `UNVERIFIED` in the tuition column where so.
- **Scholarship share / odds** — e.g. DIGICREA's often-quoted "~75% of students funded" could **not** be confirmed on the official page (it says only "a limited number per cohort"). Treat funding *rates* as indicative.
- **Eligibility fine print for Tunisians** is confirmed at the scheme level (Tunisia eligible for Erasmus Mundus, DAAD, Chevening, Stipendium Hungaricum, Türkiye Bursları, Banach, Irish IES, Fulbright). Some *national* schemes exclude Tunisia or are Balkans/ODA-restricted — those are flagged in the row (e.g. Commonwealth Master's: **not** open to Tunisia; some Australia Awards streams closed to Tunisia).
- **Portfolio/audition specifics** for arts/conservatory programs (exact format, whether a live audition vs. recorded) sometimes need confirmation per department.
