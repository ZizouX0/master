## The one number that should change how you feel

**Only 49 of 717 programmes require an audition.**

A portfolio is not an audition. An audition needs conservatoire performance training you cannot
acquire in a year. A portfolio needs **four finished pieces**, and almost every door in this file
opens with one. The admission gate is column H of `ARTIST-ROUTE.xlsx`, colour-coded, and the
exact specification — how many works, how long, what format — is a first-class column beside it.

## Closed to you — and why

Knowing these saves applications, fees and weeks.

### Closed by nationality

| Scheme | The rule |
|---|---|
| **Swiss Government Excellence — art branch** | Limited to ~26 countries with an art reciprocity agreement. **No African country is on it.** The Swiss Embassy in Tunis offers Tunisians research, PhD and postdoctoral awards only |
| **GREAT Scholarships (UK)** | The 2026 list is 15 countries. Tunisia is not among them |
| **Commonwealth (UK)** | Tunisia is not a Commonwealth country |
| **Australia Awards** | Tunisia is not a participating country — which removes the funding route from **every** Australian coursework master's |
| **Manaaki New Zealand** | Eligible countries are Pacific and Asia only. **No African country.** Aggregator sites saying otherwise are out of date |
| **Belgian fee exemption** | ARES circulaire n°2026-001 lists 44 LDCs, 20 low-HDI countries and Lebanon. Tunisia is on none. *(Tunisia* is *an ARES cooperation partner — a different instrument, for scholarships)* |
| **PAEC OEA-GCUB** | OAS member states only |
| **Israel** | A Tunisian passport is not valid for entry |

### Closed by timing

- **Chevening** requires **two years / 2,800 hours of work experience after the bachelor's**, and closes **6 October 2026** for a 2027 start. You graduate now — you cannot meet it. It also caps courses at 12 months, which kills every two-year UK conservatoire route. **Chevening becomes realistic for 2028–29.**
- **MEXT Japan** closed on 22 May 2026 in Tunis. The next call funds a **2028** start.
- **Global Korea Scholarship** would mean applying February 2027, arriving September 2027 for a **compulsory Korean-language year**, and starting the degree in **March 2028**.
- **GCUB-Mob (Brazil)** — the 2026 call closed 6 July 2026.

**STEIM Amsterdam** dissolved in 2020. **UdK Berlin Sound Studies** takes no new students. **ArtEZ "Sound of Innovation"** admits nobody from 2025-26. **UiO Music, Communication and Technology** is suspended. **Aalto "Sound in New Media"** has no 2027 intake (next 2028). **Gothenburg** has no 2027 intake. **Liepāja University** no longer exists — merged into RTU. **Newcastle's** MA Creative Arts Practice is suspended. **SAE Paris's** national-degree master is gone; what's left is a school certificate. **Le Fresnoy** requires Bac+5 or seven years' experience. **Sorbonne ATIAM** is M2-only. **Brown** is PhD-only. **NAWA Łukasiewicz** last ran in 2020. **Zagreb's** composition degree is an integrated five-year with no master's entry.

## Home, and the region

**One Tunisian master's exists in this field, and it is film sound.** ESAC Gammarth (Université de
Carthage, public, free) runs a *Mastère Professionnel en Sciences et Techniques de l'Audiovisuel
et du Cinéma, parcours **Son et mixage***. Location recording, editing, mixing, post-production —
no electronic music, no production, no creative coding. Applications run late May–June.

Everything else came back negative: ISM Tunis, Sfax and Sousse (musicology and performance only),
the Beaux-Arts institutes (visual and design), ISAMM (media engineering — accepts your degree but
does not teach your subject), and every private university. Morocco's ISMAC stops at licence and
caps entry at 25; Algeria's ISMAS masters are theatre and photography.

The structural finding is striking: **Tunisia built real undergraduate capacity in this field** —
sound engineering, music informatics and sound-recording licences at Sousse and Sfax — **and never
built a master's on top of it.**

> ⚠️ **One thing to check before any Tunisian public application:** your licence is from SMU /
> Med Tech, a **private** university. Ministerial *équivalence* must be confirmed first.

## What to do next

1. **Build the portfolio.** Four finished pieces. Every door in the verified shortlist opens with one, and none of them needs an audition. This is the only thing that gates everything.
2. **Sit IELTS.** Sonology needs 6.0, KAIST 6.5. Nothing else you can do costs less and unlocks more.
3. **Email the four unknowns** — KUG Graz (German level), HfM Karlsruhe (entry rule), Glasgow (Tunisia and the African/Caribbean waiver), and Santa Cecilia (confirm the non-music admission track for 2027).
4. **Diarise the three windows that decide 2027:** Türkiye Bursları arts **10 Jan – 20 Feb**, DIGICREA's call opening **November 2026**, and KUG Graz **1 March – 1 May 2027**.
5. **Confirm your équivalence** if you want the free Tunisian option at ESAC.
