# THE A-LIST — all 13

## 🥇 The standouts

### 1. DIGICREA — Erasmus Mundus, Digital Creativity, Art & Science 🇫🇷🇵🇹🇵🇱
**Saint-Étienne → Porto → Katowice · English · 2 yrs · ZERO tuition · €1,400/month**
> *"The only programme here that clears every one of his constraints at once."*

Tuition is **zero for everyone**, scholarship or not — confirmed verbatim on the official
financial-aid page. Sound-Music entry track. Portfolio: an artistic/professional portfolio of
productions. **Next call opens ~Nov 2026** — exactly your cycle.

### 2. ReSound — Erasmus Mundus Joint MA in Sound Design for Media Arts 🇮🇪🇵🇹🇱🇹
**Dublin → Lisbon → Vilnius · English · 2 yrs · Full + €1,400/mo**
> *"By a wide margin the best funding-to-fit ratio available to a Tunisian applicant."*

Officially accepts **computational science** degrees. Portfolio: **max 3 works, 10–15 min, one
link, each with a short reflective paragraph**. No live audition — online interview only.

### 3. KASK & Conservatorium Ghent — Master of Music, **Music Production** 🇧🇪
**Ghent · English track · Non-EEA scholarship explicitly sized to cover the fee**
> *"64 of 120 credits are literally producing."*

The most **production-dense** curriculum found in Europe. Verification *strengthened* this one.
If you want to leave with finished records rather than theory, this is the programme.

### 4. İTÜ MİAM — MA Music, **Sonic Arts** concentration 🇹🇷
**Istanbul · 100% English · ~USD 1,000/year tuition — even without a scholarship**
> *"Strongest fit in this slice and the one to build the next twelve months around."*

Verification found it **far cheaper than estimated**. Fully English-taught, and Türkiye Bursları
can make it free entirely. Extraordinary value.

### 5. University of Glasgow — MSc Creative Sound Design & Audiovisual Media 🇬🇧
**Glasgow · English · 12 months · £26,460 — African Excellence Award = full tuition waiver**

Entry list **names computing outright**. Portfolio: only **two or three examples**. No audition.
Best risk-adjusted UK option.

## ⛔ Verified as blocked — don't waste applications

- **Filmuniversität Babelsberg** (Sound Design for Audiovisual Media) — needs a **B.F.A. in Sound or
  Tonmeister**, **DSH-1 German**, a reel of **three films you personally mixed**, and an on-site
  **Pro Tools scoring exam**. Its 2027 window is real (12 Apr–18 May 2027) but it's closed to you,
  and it trains cinema sound supervisors, not producers.
- **HfM Detmold Musikregie/Tonmeister** — a 1-year top-up *on an existing Tonmeister bachelor*.
- Conservatoire performance masters generally — live auditions assume training you don't have.

## What changed after verification

1. **KASK Ghent jumped to the top.** 64 of 120 credits are production. It's the most craft-dense
   option in Europe and its non-EEA scholarship is sized to cover the fee.
2. **İTÜ Istanbul is ~USD 1,000/year** — an order of magnitude cheaper than I'd estimated, fully in
   English, and fundable to zero via Türkiye Bursları.
3. **ARTS² Mons is a French-language route worth taking seriously** — €1,194/yr, and your French
   fluency makes it accessible in a way it wouldn't be for most applicants.
4. **Santa Cecilia Rome's entry bar is much lower than assumed** for a non-music graduate.
5. **Babelsberg is definitively out** — the best film-sound school in Europe, and genuinely closed
   to your profile.
