## THE HEADLINE FINDS

### 1. Politecnico di Milano — MSc Music and Acoustic Engineering *(Music Engineering track)* 🇮🇹
**Milan · English · 2 years / 120 CFU · ~€4,000/yr · merit scholarships (auto-considered)**

**The single easiest admission on this entire list — and it's cheap.**
- **No portfolio. No audition.** Admission is on transcript + English certificate alone. Given that
  the portfolio is your main gate everywhere else, this matters enormously.
- It doesn't merely *accept* a CS degree — it **prefers** one. Official wording targets
  *"graduates in Computer Science and Engineering"*.
- Studies: Advanced Coding Tools, Computer Music — Representations and Models, Multimedia Signal
  Processing, Sound Analysis/Synthesis/Processing, Sound Design.
- **Honest caveat:** this is *music engineering*, not producer craft. You'd leave able to build
  audio software and understand sound deeply — not necessarily with a finished EP. It's Path 1
  wearing a Path 2 badge. Best treated as your **safety school with real funding upside**.
- `polimi.it/en/education/laurea-magistrale-programmes/programme-detail/music-a…`

### 2. University of Glasgow — MSc Creative Sound Design & Audiovisual Media 🇬🇧
**Glasgow · English · 12 months · £26,460 — BUT full tuition waiver available**

**Probably the best risk-adjusted craft option in the UK for you.**
- **African Excellence Award — a FULL TUITION FEE WAIVER, up to 16 awards**, for international-fee
  students. Tunisia is African; confirm eligibility directly, this is the crux.
- Entry list explicitly names **"electronics, computing or acoustics"** — your degree is inside the
  stated profile, not an exception to it.
- Portfolio is small: **normally two or three pieces**. No audition.
- Studies: Sound Shaping and Design · Creative Sound Recording · Audiovisual Composition ·
  Creative Sound & Media Performance. Genuinely production- and performance-led.
- `gla.ac.uk/postgraduate/taught/creative-sound-design-audiovisual-media/`

### 3. Aalto University — MA Sound in New Media 🇫🇮
**Espoo/Helsinki · English · 2 years / 120 ECTS · €20,000/yr — Aalto Scholarship covers 50% or 100%**

- **No music degree required, no instrument audition, no on-site test.** Only a "relevant Bachelor's".
- Selection is **portfolio + interview**, and the portfolio explicitly rewards a
  concept-and-technology approach — your coding is an asset, not a curiosity.
- Studies: concept design → live performance → storytelling → programming. Art-school framing with
  real technical depth.
- **Best risk-adjusted Nordic option.** `aalto.fi/study-options/masters-programme-in-new-media-sound-in-new-media`

### 4. Aalborg University Copenhagen — MSc Medialogy, Sound & Music Computing 🇩🇰
**Copenhagen · English · 2 years · ~€15,500/yr — ~15 full scholarships/yr (waiver + stipend)**

- Qualifying bachelors **explicitly include "Software"** — built for your exact background.
- **No portfolio, no audition.**
- ~15 AAU scholarships per year = full tuition waiver **plus** monthly stipend. Real odds, not a lottery.
- `en.aau.dk/education/master/medialogy-cph`

### 5. Leeds Conservatoire — MA Music Production 🇬🇧
**Leeds · English · 1 year · £20,600 · £5,000 Principal's Scholarships (×4 for internationals)**

**The most accessible genuinely production-focused master found.**
- Quoted verbatim: *"qualifications in other fields will also be reviewed and considered"*.
- **IELTS only 6.0** — the lowest bar on this list, and you haven't sat the test yet.
- Portfolio: **two projects, ~10 minutes** — studio production, sound design or electronic composition.
- Modules: Creative Music Production · Postproduction Mixing and Mastering (**includes Dolby Atmos**)
  · Major Project. This is craft, not theory.
- `leedsconservatoire.ac.uk/courses/postgraduate-courses/ma-music-production/`

### 6. Goldsmiths, University of London — MMus Creative Practice *(Popular Music Composition & Production)* 🇬🇧
**London · English · 1 year · £22,000 — departmental full-fee-waiver scholarship exists**

- Entry clause openly accommodates you: *"you might also be considered… if your degree is in an
  unrelated field, but have relevant experience"*.
- Portfolio: **four to six pieces** of recent original creative work. No audition.
- The most electronic-music-friendly MMus in London, in the best city in Europe for club music.
- `gold.ac.uk/pg/mmus-creative-practice/`

### 7. Trinity College Dublin — M.Phil. Music and Media Technologies 🇮🇪
**Dublin · English · 1 year / 90 ECTS**

- Requires a 2.1 *"preferably, but not necessarily, in music, **engineering or science**"* — you're
  in the preferred set.
- Portfolio accepts *"musical/compositional/artistic **and/or technological** ability"* — **a coding
  project can be your portfolio piece.** Very few programmes say this out loud.
- Modules: Electroacoustic Composition · Sonic Arts · Creative Coding · Audio Engineering.
- Pair with the **Government of Ireland IES** scholarship (full tuition + €10,000).
- `tcd.ie/courses/postgraduate/courses/music-and-media-technologies-mphil/`

### 8. Berklee Valencia — MM Music Production, Technology & Innovation 🇪🇸
**Valencia · English · 1 year · ~$50,430 + fees — scholarships exist but partial**

- The strongest pure **producer/electronic-artist** curriculum found: hybrid analogue/digital
  recording, studio production, **electronic music production AND performance**, Max/MSP.
- *"Bachelor's degrees in other disciplines will be considered"*. **No audition** — instead a video
  describing three projects you're passionate about.
- **The blocker is money.** Even with a scholarship this is the most expensive option here by far.
- `valencia.berklee.edu/graduate-programs/mpti/`

## AVOID — verified as blocked for you

- **Filmuniversität Babelsberg — Sound Design for Audiovisual Media** *(fully verified)*. Europe's
  best film-sound school, and the 2027 window is genuinely published (12 Apr – 18 May 2027). But it
  requires a **B.F.A. in Sound or Tonmeister**, **DSH-1 German**, a reel of **at least three films
  you personally mixed**, and an on-site **Pro Tools scoring exam**. It also trains cinema sound
  supervisors, not producers. *Do not spend application effort here.*
- **HfM Detmold — Musikregie/Tonmeister** *(verified)*: a 1-year top-up **on an existing Tonmeister
  bachelor**. Not a conversion route.
- Conservatoire performance masters generally — live auditions assume training you don't have.

## What changed in my advice

Three things this sweep found that I'd have missed:

1. **Politecnico di Milano needs no portfolio at all.** Every other route makes the portfolio the
   gate. This one doesn't, it's cheap, and it explicitly wants CS graduates. It belongs on your list
   as a high-probability funded option even though the craft is more technical than you want.
2. **Glasgow's African Excellence Award** is a full tuition waiver with up to 16 slots, on a
   programme whose entry list names *computing*. That combination is rare.
3. **Leeds Conservatoire asks IELTS 6.0 and a 10-minute portfolio**, and puts *"other fields
   considered"* in writing. It's the lowest-friction real production master found.
