# Visa letter before 1 December 2026 — English, in-person programmes starting January–March 2027

**Checked 27 September 2026** — nine searches over all Spanish universities (public and private),
authorised foreign centres, directories, and a school-by-school timing check. Only institutions that
are valid for a student visa are listed (universities, or foreign centres authorised in the RUCT register).

> **No university can guarantee the letter in advance** — it first has to admit you. What matters is
> how fast it decides and issues the letter after you pay. **Apply to 2–3 at once in the first week of
> October**, with a complete file, and write in every application that you need the letter by
> 30 November 2026 for your visa.

## 2. What to do now

1. **Get your degree and transcript apostilled and translated now** — UCAM will not issue the letter
   without it, and UPC and most others need it too. It is usually the slowest step.
2. **First week of October**, apply to 2–3 in parallel. Cheapest realistic combination:
   - **UPC** — if your bachelor's is engineering/science/maths/computing: MAMME (from 16 Oct),
     Structural & Construction (7–21 Oct), MAST/MASTEAM/AI4CI or MATT/MET/MEE (from ~5 Oct).
     Email the school asking for an early decision "for VISA procedure deadlines".
   - **UCAM International Relations** — any bachelor's. `admissions@ucam.edu`, +34 968 27 82 90.
   - **La Salle** (1 March) — €1,000, the most time for the visa.
3. Pay the moment you are admitted, and ask for the letter as a PDF by email.
