## Tier 1 — Best overall: full funding + strong fit + accepts your CS background

### 1. DIGICREA — Erasmus Mundus Joint Master in Digital Creativity, Art & Science 🇫🇷🇵🇱🇵🇹
- **Funding:** FULL — €1,400/month × 24 months (max €33,600) **and zero tuition even without the scholarship** → lowest-risk option on this list.
- **Fit:** Sound-Music specialization (compositions, sound design, music). Leverages your software background at the art/tech/science intersection.
- **Timing (ideal):** 2027–2029 cohort call **opens ~November 2026** — exactly your cycle. Portfolio of 3–5 works + motivation letter + 2 references. English B2+ (IELTS 6 / TOEFL 90).
- **Source:** master-digicrea.univ-st-etienne.fr

### 2. ReSound — Erasmus Mundus Joint Master in Sound (Sound Design for Media Arts) 🇮🇪🇵🇹🇱🇹
- **Funding:** FULL EMJM — tuition waiver + ~€1,400/month living allowance + travel/visa (a share of the 24 places).
- **Fit:** *Best pure sound-design fit.* Official entry list explicitly names **"computational science"** backgrounds. Builds sound design/production/composition for film, games, immersive.
- **Timing:** prior cycle opened 20 Oct 2025, closed 27 Jan 2026 → expect **~Oct 2026–Jan 2027**. Portfolio: max 3 works, 10–15 min. IELTS 6.0 / TOEFL 79.
- **Source:** iadt.ie/courses/erasmus-mundus-joint-ma-resound

### 3. MSc Interactive Media Technology — Sound & Music Computing 🇸🇪 (KTH, Stockholm)
- **Funding:** KTH Scholarship (full tuition) for fee-paying applicants; or Swedish Institute SISGP (full tuition + living — but needs ~3,000h work experience, likely doesn't fit you yet — **use the KTH Scholarship route**).
- **Fit:** Software engineering fits the entry profile directly; **Sound & Music Computing is an explicit specialization**. The most CS-aligned audio program here.
- **Timing (CONFIRMED real dates):** opens **16 Oct 2026**, deadline **15 Jan 2027**, decisions 1 Apr 2027. No portfolio. IELTS/TOEFL required.
- **Source:** kth.se (MSc Interactive Media Technology)

### 4. MSc Medialogy (Sound & Music Computing focus) 🇩🇰 (Aalborg University)
- **Funding:** FULL (competitive) — Danish Government Scholarship = full tuition + **~€1,503/month** stipend (≈15/yr across all programs, automatic consideration).
- **Fit:** CS-friendly interactive/media technology with sound & music computing; software background is an asset.
- **Timing:** deadline **1 March** (for September intake); portal opens ~January. IELTS/TOEFL required.
- **Source:** en.aau.dk/education/master/medialogy

### 5. MSc (Tech) Acoustics and Audio Technology 🇫🇮 (Aalto University)
- **Funding:** FULL — Aalto tuition-fee-waiver scholarship for top applicants.
- **Fit:** Leverages your CS/maths hard (audio DSP, signal processing). More engineering than production, but the deepest technical-audio option.
- **Timing:** prior cycle Dec 1 2025 – Jan 2 2026 → expect **~Dec 2026–early Jan 2027**. Requires university-level maths (linear algebra, Fourier). No portfolio.
- **Source:** aalto.fi (Acoustics and Audio Technology MSc)

## Tier 2 — Big government scholarships (fund a master of your choice)

### 6. Chevening Scholarship 🇬🇧 (fully funded, any 1-year UK master)
- **Funding:** FULL — tuition + living + flights + visa. Portable: pair with a UK music-tech master (Surrey, QMUL C4DM) or a music-business MA.
- **Timing (CONFIRMED):** deadline **6 October 2026, 11:00 UTC** for 2027 entry — **this is one of your earliest hard deadlines.** Opens ~early Aug 2026. Essays + references + interview; weighted to leadership/work experience.
- **Source:** chevening.org/scholarship/tunisia

### 7. DAAD Study Scholarship — Master Studies 🇩🇪 (fund a German music/audio master)
- **Funding:** FULL — €992/month + insurance + travel + study allowance. Pair with e.g. TU Berlin *Audio Communication and Technology*.
- **Note:** purely artistic programs are routed to a separate DAAD scheme; a tech-leaning audio MSc fits this one. Degree normally ≤6 years old (you qualify).
- **Timing:** application window opens 1 June annually; Tunisia deadline typically autumn. IELTS/TOEFL per program.
- **Source:** daad.de scholarship database

### 8. Stipendium Hungaricum 🇭🇺 (fully funded, incl. music/sound-tech masters)
- **Funding:** FULL — tuition + monthly stipend + free dormitory/housing contribution + insurance. **Tunisia has a dedicated eligibility page** → realistic odds; one of your better-value safety options *with* funding.
- **Timing:** prior cycle closed 15 Jan 2026 → expect **opens ~mid-Nov 2026, closes ~15 Jan 2027.** Dual application (DreamApply + Tunisian ministry nomination). Music programs usually need audition/portfolio video.
- **Source:** stipendiumhungaricum.hu/country/tunisia

### 9. Türkiye Bursları → ITU MIAM, MA Music (Sonic Arts / Sound Engineering & Design) 🇹🇷
- **Funding:** FULL — tuition + monthly stipend + accommodation + flights + insurance.
- **Fit:** MIAM is Turkey's first **100% English** MA music program; Sonic Arts / Sound Engineering & Design concentrations are strong for your goal.
- **Timing (CONFIRMED recurring window):** **10 Jan – 20 Feb 2027.** Under-30 rule for master's. Portfolio/interview likely.
- **Source:** miam.itu.edu.tr

### 10. NAWA Banach Scholarship 🇵🇱 (fully funded, any Polish public master)
- **Funding:** FULL — PLN 2,500/month + tuition exemption + travel + prep course. **Tunisia explicitly listed (Country Group 3).** Pair with AGH (sound engineering) or a music-tech master.
- **Timing:** 2026 call closed 8 May → expect **~April 2027 call.** Must not already hold a master's (you don't). Selection is GPA + short video (no portfolio).
- **Source:** nawa.gov.pl (Banach Programme)

### 11. Government of Ireland International Education Scholarship 🇮🇪 (fund any Irish taught master)
- **Funding:** FULL — €10,000 stipend + full tuition waiver (~60 awards/yr). Pair with e.g. University of Limerick Irish World Academy, or an Irish music-tech master.
- **Timing:** prior call closed 12 Mar 2026 → expect **~Jan–Mar 2027** (well-timed for Sept 2027). Apply to the master first, then the scholarship.
- **Source:** hea.ie/policy/internationalisation/goi-ies

### 12. Fulbright Foreign Student Program 🇺🇸 (fund a full US master)
- **Funding:** FULL — tuition + living + travel. Pair with NYU, Berklee, Georgia Tech Music Technology, etc.
- ⚠️ **Timing caveat:** the 2027-entry cycle already **closed 30 June 2026**. Realistically this is a **2028 target**, not Sept 2027 — next cycle opens ~April 2027. Keep it on your radar as a fallback year.
- **Source:** amideast.org/fulbright-foreign-student-program-for-tunisia

## Tier 3 — Music-business track + best-fit programs (worth it even without full funding)

### 13. MAGMa — Erasmus Mundus, Managing Art & Cultural Heritage in Global Markets 🇬🇧🇵🇹🇫🇷🇳🇱
- **Funding:** FULL EMJM (max €33,600 over 2 yrs) — tuition + travel + insurance + monthly stipend. Your best-funded **music/arts-business** option (Glasgow + Lisbon + Paris IESA + Rotterdam).
- **Timing:** prior EM deadline 19 Jan 2026 → expect **opens ~Oct 2026.** Needs a 2:1-equivalent degree; other fields accepted with relevant experience. No portfolio.
- **Source:** gla.ac.uk/postgraduate/erasmusmundus/magma

### 14. Master of Music — Music Design 🇳🇱 (HKU Utrecht) — *the best pure fit*
- **Funding:** PARTIAL only (Holland Scholarship ~€5,000 one-off). Not fully funded — pair with external funding or budget for it.
- **Fit:** **The single best match to your goal** — combinable strands in **Music Production, Sound Design, Music Technology**; explicitly accepts non-music (e.g. software) bachelors with a strong portfolio.
- **Timing:** opens **1 October** (annual); rolling upload rounds through spring; non-EEA advised to apply early. Portfolio mandatory.
- **Source:** hku.nl (Master of Music — Music Design pathway)

### 15. Master of Music in Sonology 🇳🇱 (Institute of Sonology, Royal Conservatoire The Hague)
- **Funding:** PARTIAL (NL Scholarship €5,000, nomination-based) — tuition ~€11,488/yr non-EEA. Pair with external funding.
- **Fit:** World-famous computer/electronic-music institute that **explicitly admits computer-science graduates** and selects **on portfolio with no live audition** — an elite CS-to-music route.
- **Timing:** prior deadline 26 Feb 2026 → expect **~Feb 2027.** Portfolio + 2-page study plan. IELTS 6.0 / TOEFL 80.
- **Source:** koncon.nl (Master Sonology)
