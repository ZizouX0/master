# Cheapest English-taught private master's in Spain — with a visa-valid acceptance letter

**Checked 27 September 2026** by five searches: Madrid, Catalonia, the rest of Spain, foreign-degree
schools, and a deep check of the cheapest. Everything was confirmed on official pages. Prices are
2026-27 (2027-28 is not published yet). Starts are from **January 2027 onward**.

## The rules that decide which schools count

- **Who can give a visa-valid master's:** only **universities** (public or private) and **higher arts
  schools**. A non-university school may teach a university's master's only if the university
  certifies the agreement. A foreign degree counts only if the school is **authorised in Spain**
  (listed in the Ministry's RUCT register). *(Criterios de gestión, 23 Jun 2025, §8, §9, §13)*
- **What you pay for the letter:** by law, admission plus the **registration/enrolment fee** — not the
  whole tuition. Each school decides how much that is. *(RD 1155/2024 art. 53.1.a)*
- **Hours:** as a guide, **≥ 20 hours a week**. Shorter, intensive programmes need **≥ 12 hours a week
  in person**. *(Criterios §4)*
- **Timing:** apply for the visa **at least 2 months before classes start**.

## 3. Recommendation

1. **Cheapest of all — and it starts in January:** **UCAM, Master in International Relations**,
   Murcia. €5,989 for the year, taught in English on campus, and UCAM is a university, so it counts
   for the visa. UCAM issues the letter **no earlier than 3 months before the start**, and you must
   file the visa **2 months before** — for January 2027 that means **acting now (October 2026)**.
   Email `admissions@ucam.edu` today and ask:
   - Is there a January 2027 edition, and is it in person?
   - How many hours a week?
   - How much is the reservation fee for international students?
   - Is it refunded if the visa is refused?
   - Does the "homologation / UNED credential" step apply to master's applicants?
2. **Cheapest with published full-time hours:** **UCAM Bilingual Education** (official, ~25 h/week,
   €5,989) — November 2027.
3. **Safest deposit:** **Universidad Europea** — €1,200, fully refunded if the visa is refused
   (Valencia from €11,200, Madrid from €13,000).

*(Public comparison: UPC Barcelona's official English engineering masters start February 2027 at
€2,700 tuition, €330 for the letter — see [SPRING-INTAKE.md](./SPRING-INTAKE.md).)*
