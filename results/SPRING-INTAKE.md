# Spring 2027 (January–June start) — every English-taught, in-person master's found in Spain

**Checked 27 September 2026.** This combines five searches: Madrid, Catalonia, the rest of Spain,
public universities, and study directories (Mastersportal, FindAMasters, educations.com and others).
Every start date was checked against the institution's own page, because summary tools had invented
February intakes before.

> **The cheapest by far is a public university: UPC in Barcelona.** It runs 9–10 official master's
> taught in English that start in **February 2027**. You pay **€330 to get the visa letter**, and
> tuition is **€2,700** for a 60-ECTS master (non-EU price). The catch: these are
> engineering/science/maths/computing masters, and the admission results can come as late as
> 15 January.

## 2. About UPC — the cheapest option

- **Tuition:** €45 per ECTS for non-EU students on the 2026-27 fee page. ⚠️ The UPC FAQ still shows
  an old €102.52/ECTS figure — confirm which applies before paying.
- **February places:** MATT 20 · MASTEAM 10 · MET 20 · MEE 20 · MAMME 3. The others are not stated.
  ESEIAAT (Technology & Engineering Management) offered **no** February 2026 places.
- **Dates:** pre-enrolment **5 Oct – 9 Dec 2026**; each school opens a shorter window inside this. Last
  year ESEIAAT's non-EU window was 20 Oct – 28 Nov. Admitted lists come out **by 15 Jan 2027** and
  enrolment is 25 Jan – 12 Feb. Classes start around 15 Feb.
- **Visa timing risk:** if the letter only comes in January, there is very little time for the visa.
  Apply on the **first day** the school's window opens, and ask the school for an early decision
  for non-EU applicants.
- **Entry requirement:** a bachelor's in a relevant engineering, science, maths or computing field.
  MASTEAM and MIRI need English B2. MIRI publishes 20 h/week of lectures.
- Sources: [MATT](https://upc.edu/en/masters/advanced-telecommunication-technologies) ·
  [fees](https://www.upc.edu/en/masters/fees-grants) ·
  [calendar](https://www.upc.edu/en/masters/academic-calendar-and-academic-regulations) ·
  [pre-enrolment](https://www.upc.edu/en/masters/access-admission-enrolment/pre-enrolment) ·
  [€300 advance](https://www.upc.edu/en/masters/access-admission-enrolment/enrolment) ·
  [FAQ](https://www.upc.edu/en/masters/access-admission-enrolment/frequently-asked-questions) ·
  [MASTEAM](https://masteam.masters.upc.edu/en/admission/information)

## 3. Recommendation

1. **Cheapest:** **UPC** — €330 for the letter, about €3,100 in total for a 60-ECTS master. Only if
   your bachelor fits one of the programmes. Apply the day the window opens (from 5 October).
2. **Cheapest with a refund-safe deposit:** **La Salle** (official, 1 March) — lose only €300. Or
   **Elisava** (February) — everything refunded, but the deadline is mid-November.
3. **Most time for the visa:** April starts — **UNIE** (official, 20 h/week; ask its price),
   Harbour.Space (26 Apr), EU Business School (5 Apr), IE (20 Apr).
