# Spring 2027 intake — English-taught, in-person master's at private schools in Spain

**Checked 27 September 2026** on official pages.

> **Short answer:** only a few start between January and April 2027. **Nothing near Alicante has a
> spring intake** — UCAM, Universidad Europea (apart from one basketball master's), UCV, ENAE, UA and
> UMH all start in September–November only. The spring options are in **Madrid and Barcelona**.

## Timing — you need to move now

- For a **February–March** start: get admitted in **October–November 2026** and file the visa in
  **November–December**. Schiller advises applying about 4 months before the start, i.e. by early
  October.
- **January** starts are probably too tight now.
- The **April** starts (EU Business School 5 Apr, GBSB 12 Apr) leave the most time.
- Elisava's non-EU deadline for the February 2026 intake was 16 November 2025, so expect **mid-November
  2026** for February 2027. Confirm with them.

## Recommendation

1. **Cheapest:** **UCJC, Madrid** — official, €6,900. But the deposit isn't refunded and the
   afternoon/weekend timetable is a visa risk, so ask UCJC in writing whether its non-EU students on
   the February edition got visas.
2. **Best balance:** **La Salle, Barcelona** — official, starts 1 March, €12,660; you lose only
   €300 if the visa is refused.
3. **Safest money:** **Elisava, Barcelona** — February, €12,900, and everything is refunded if the
   visa is refused.
