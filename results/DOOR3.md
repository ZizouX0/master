## The thing to understand before reading the list

**A practical production test is not an audition.**

Door 3 programmes gate in three different ways, and they are not equally hard for you:

| Gate | What it means | Can you pass it? |
|---|---|---|
| **Portfolio** | Submit 2–4 finished tracks | **Yes** — this is a twelve-month build |
| **Production test** | *"Here is raw material, make a production"* | **Yes** — this is the job, not a barrier |
| **Ear-training / performance audition** | Play an instrument, identify intervals, sight-read | **No** — this needs conservatoire training |

Only **14 of 261** demand a performance audition outright. The rest are reachable.

### The German Tonmeister degrees are closed — but not for the reason I first gave

Verification overturned this. **Detmold's** master aptitude test is *three of your own productions
on CD/DVD plus a 30-minute colloquium* — **no ear test, no audition**. The **Royal Danish Academy**
runs **no live entrance exam at all**: two productions of 7+ minutes and a 20-minute discussion.
**CNSMD Paris** gates on the conservatoire *DEM*.

What closes them is a **prerequisite degree** — Detmold demands a *"Bachelor of Music
Musikübertragung bzw. Diplom-Tonmeister"*, Copenhagen a *"Bachelor of Music as tonemeister"* —
plus, at Detmold, German C1. The ear tests live on the **bachelor** entrance, not the master's.
Only **FAMU** and **Robert Schumann Düsseldorf** genuinely gate on musicianship.

That distinction matters: a closed prerequisite degree is sometimes negotiable by equivalence.
A trained ear is not something you acquire in a year.

> Two things that look like ear tests and are not: Novi Sad's *аудиограм* and mdw Vienna's
> *"Nachweis der einwandfreien Gehörfähigkeit"* are **medical hearing certificates**.

## The free ones, and why most of them are still closed

Thirteen European Door-3 degrees cost you nothing. Only three are a realistic chance:

**Possible for you**
- **HfMT Köln — Production (M.Mus.)** — €0, German **A2**, portfolio is **two audio files** from the
  last two years plus documentation of your working method. The entrance test is *"candidates
  receive raw material and must create a production."* The single best free option in this door.
- **HAW Hamburg — Zeitabhängige Medien / Sound–Vision (M.A.)** — €0, German, project-centred study
  across sound and moving image.
- **Filmuniversität Babelsberg — M.A. Tonmeister für audiovisuelle Medien** — €0, German.

**Free but rated Weak — the Tonmeister wall**
Detmold (Erich-Thienhaus-Institut, four separate records), Robert Schumann Düsseldorf, Babelsberg's
other sound tracks, and FAMU Prague. These are excellent, famous, free — and every one of them
assumes instrumental ability and ear training. They are the reason "free" is not the same as
"reachable."

## The 130 that are not degrees

A third of everything sold as a master's in this door is not one — the highest proportion
of any field this project has searched. Door 3 is where the private audio-school market lives.

The pattern by country: **Netherlands** — Abbey Road Institute Amsterdam, SAE Amsterdam, DJ School
Amsterdam, Dutch Dance Music Academy, Herman Brood Academie, Master The Mix, Music Production
Academy, Pro Audio Education NL, Sound Education Nederland. **Spain** — Microfusa, CEV, CICE, Aula
Creactiva, Point Blank Ibiza, SAE Spain, and *título propio* programmes at TAI, UCAM and Francisco
de Vitoria. **Italy** — Accademia del Suono, SAE Milano, Scuola Mohole, and IULM's *Master di I
livello*. **Portugal** — ETIC, Restart, DJEM, EMMA. **France** — ESIS, EICAR, CinéCréatis, MJM and ATLA all sell a *"Mastère"* that is **RNCP level 5
or 6**, bachelor level or below; **3iS** likewise, at €15,000/yr for two years.

**The UK validation chain, now traced:** **ICMP awards its own degrees** — full degree-awarding
powers since November 2021, so it is *not* a validated provider. But **SAE Glasgow's
identically-named MA is awarded by the University of Hertfordshire.** The rest: **dBs → Falmouth**,
**Thinkspace → AUB**, **Leeds Conservatoire → Hull**, **ACM → Middlesex**. **Point Blank has no
postgraduate provision at all.** And **dBs accepts international students on its online programmes
only** — which means **no student visa**.

They are all on the **NOT a degree — avoid** tab with the reason for each. Read it before you pay
anyone.

## Money — 49 schemes, and the pattern behind them

**Nationality was rarely the bar. Subject was.** Eiffel excludes the arts, NAWA Banach is STEM-only
(Tunisia *is* listed), the Islamic Development Bank funds science and engineering, the OPEC Fund
funds development. **Your software-engineering BSc fits all four — the master's subject is what
fails them.**

**The eight worth pursuing**

| Scheme | What it pays | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|
| **Tunisian *bourse d'études à l'étranger*** (best.rnu.tn) | Tuition + **€800/month** + social security + health insurance + travel | Your own government — **no nationality bar at all**. Two unknowns decide it: whether arts and music are eligible fields, and whether an admission letter is needed at application. Circulaire publishes ~October 2026 |
| **Italian MAECI AFAM scholarship** | **€10,800** + medical insurance | Article 1.2 **explicitly names AFAM music courses**, and it **stacks with the regional DSU grant**. Age cap 28. Deadline ~26 March |
| **AESEF** (Audio Engineering Society Educational Foundation) | ~USD 2–5k, plus the **Dolby Institute** and **L-Acoustics** awards on the same form | The L-Acoustics award **invites applicants whose undergraduate degree was "in an area other than audio."** No admission required, no work experience, no IELTS, worldwide. Deadline 15 May |
| **ICMP Andrew Scheps Scholarship** | **Full tuition**, MSc Audio Engineering and Music Technology | No nationality clause. Judged on portfolio and video, not transcript |
| **Romanian MFA scholarships** | Tuition + stipend | No nationality list, all fields, **no admission-first requirement**, application platform in French. Deadline 31 March |
| **UAL International Postgraduate Scholarship** | £10,000 (a £50,000 tier exists) | Tunisia's presence on the eligible list is unconfirmed |
| **Huddersfield PG Merit + General Entry** | £4,000–5,000 | **Automatic and non-competitive**, on an on-subject MSc |
| **Estonian bilateral (€350/mo) + EAMT's 25–50% waiver** | Together, workable | **Tunisia is named on Harno's list**. Only viable as the pair |

**Hidden work-experience bars** that would disqualify you: **Popakademie** needs six months'
industry experience plus C1 German · **CNM** needs French tax residence and regular professional
activity in France · **PRS Foundation** needs a UK base and an 18-month track record ·
**Help Musicians** needs three years living and studying in the UK, and excludes music technology
outright · **ICMP's own** PG scholarship needs three years' UK/EEA residency · **Fundación SGAE**
requires existing membership.

**Erasmus+ International Credit Mobility cannot fund a full master's.** It caps physical mobility at
12 months, is defined as credit mobility inside ongoing studies, and requires you to remain enrolled
at a Tunisian institution. It does not belong in your plan.

**Country schemes checked and closed:** Latvia and Czechia exclude Tunisia. Greece's postgraduate
stream is not open. Iceland funds Icelandic studies only. Portugal (Camões) and Spain (MAEC-AECID)
have no live foreign master's line. Lithuania, Croatia, Serbia and Bulgaria **publish no retrievable
country list at all** — genuinely unknown rather than negative. Abbey Road requires a UK passport;
SSL is UK-based and undergraduate.

**Earliest 2027 deadlines:** Eiffel and Prince Claus **8 January** · IsDB **31 January** ·
**RMC Copenhagen 3 February** · MAECI **26 March** · Romania **31 March** · NAWA **early May**
(country quotas can close it weeks early) · AESEF **15 May**. Watch from **autumn 2026**: the
Tunisian circulaire, and UAL's 2027/28 international applications opening.

**One deadline is nearly here: the Royal Danish Academy closes 1 December 2026 for autumn 2027.**
