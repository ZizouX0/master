# Cheapest visa acceptance letter — private university, English, in person, January–March 2027 start

**Checked 27 September 2026.** Five searches covered all of Spain: Madrid, Catalonia, UCAM and its
partners, the rest of Spain, and authorised foreign-degree centres plus study directories.

> **Result:** **no programme is confirmed at €1,000 or less.** Three come close. The cheapest
> *possible* routes are **UCAM (€430 if it waives the reservation)** and **Universidad Europea /
> Real Madrid (~€750, only if booked by 30 September 2026)** — both must be confirmed with the
> university.

## 2. A possible €0 route — allowed by the Ministry's guidance

*Criterios de gestión* (23 June 2025), §7:

> *"Es suficiente acreditar el abono de los derechos de inscripción o matrícula, no de la totalidad
> del curso… si se acredita alguna de estas circunstancias, o, en su caso, se aporta un compromiso de
> efectuar el abono o si se ha admitido como tal la matrícula (lo cual se verifica mediante un
> certificado expedido por el centro educativo), se entiende cumplido el requisito."*

In plain words: instead of paying, you can show **a signed promise to pay**, or **a certificate from
the university that your enrolment is accepted**. Ask each university whether it will issue that.
Note that some consulates' own checklists (e.g. Casablanca: *"sin el pago de la matrícula no se
recogerá el visado"*) still insist on a payment receipt — check yours.

## 3. What to ask

**UCAM** — `admissions@ucam.edu`:
> For the Master in International Relations, January 2027 intake: (1) Is the €1,000 place reservation
> required before the admission letter, or can I get the letter after only the €150 application and
> €280 pre-inscription fees? (2) Can you issue a certificate that my enrolment is accepted, with a
> payment commitment? (3) Is the reservation deducted from the €5,500 tuition? (4) How many hours a
> week, and is the January 2027 edition confirmed in person?

**Universidad Europea — Real Madrid** — **+34 918 257 527**, before 30 September: the exact
2026/27 reservation fee with the 50 % discount, the tuition, the weekly hours, and whether the
admission letter is issued right after the reservation.

**La Salle** — is the reservation €1,000 or €1,500 for the 1 March 2027 intake?
