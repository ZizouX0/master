# Private Spanish universities — English-taught master's with a cheap deposit and a visa letter

**Checked 26–27 September 2026** on official university pages and PDFs. Fees are for **2026-27**;
nobody has published 2027-28 master's fees yet, so expect small rises.

---

## 1. What the visa letter actually needs — this decides what "cheap" can buy

| Rule | What it says | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **You need admission + a paid enrolment fee — not the whole tuition** | RD 1155/2024 art. 53.1.a: *"Haber sido admitido… Haber abonado los derechos de inscripción, matrícula o documento equivalente."* The Ministry's June 2025 criteria: *"Es suficiente acreditar el abono de los derechos de inscripción o matrícula, no de la totalidad del curso."* | [BOE](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2024-24099) · [Criterios de gestión, 23 Jun 2025](https://www.arc-legal.es/storage/2025/06/ARC-Legal-Criterios-Estancias-por-Estudios.pdf) |
| **A letter bought with only an application fee is risky** | Some consulates want proof the enrolment was paid. Nador: *"no es válida la mera reserva de plaza"*. Casablanca: *"SIN EL PAGO DE LA MATRÍCULA NO SE RECOGERÁ EL VISADO"*. Rabat / Algiers: *"la admisión y el pago de la matrícula"*. New Delhi is softer: *"proof of payment of the fees **or reservation** of the course"* | [Rabat](https://www.exteriores.gob.es/Consulados/rabat/es/ServiciosConsulares/Paginas/index.aspx?scca=Visados&scco=Marruecos&scd=6&scs=Visados+Nacionales+-+Visado+de+estudios) · [Casablanca PDF](https://www.exteriores.gob.es/Consulados/casablanca/es/Consulado/PublishingImages/Paginas/Visados/REQUISITOS%20VISADO%20ESTUDIOS.pdf) · [Nador PDF](https://www.exteriores.gob.es/Consulados/nador/es/Comunicacion/Noticias/Documents/NOTA%20INFORMATIVA%20VISADOS%20DE%20ESTUDIOS-2.pdf) · [New Delhi PDF](https://www.exteriores.gob.es/Embajadas/nuevadelhi/en/ServiciosConsulares/Documents/STUDY%20VISA%20FOR%20MORE%20THAN%2090%20DAYS.pdf) |
| **Full-time, in person** | You must be enrolled in ≥ 90 % of the year's credits (art. 52.2). As a *guide*, **≥ 20 teaching hours a week**; shorter intensive programmes need **≥ 12 h/week in person** (Criterios §4). Many English evening masters are only 12–16 h/week — see the ⚠️ flags below | Criterios §4 |
| **Non-official masters (*títulos propios*) are accepted for the visa** | *"Ambos son admisibles"* (Criterios §8). They are real university programmes but **not official degrees** — fine for the visa, weaker for recognition or a PhD later | Criterios §8 |
| **The letter should state** | programme name, start and end dates, duration, weekly hours, that it is in-person and full-time, and the amount you paid | Casablanca / Nador checklists |
| **Money you must show** | 100 % IPREM = **€600 / month** (€7,200 for 12 months) for living costs. Money set aside for tuition does **not** count toward it (art. 35.h) | [BOE](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2024-24099) |
| **Health insurance** | Full cover, no co-payments, no waiting period, from an insurer authorised in Spain | Criterios §6 |
| **When to apply** | At least 2 months before the course starts (art. 36.1); there is no "too early" limit | BOE / Criterios §1 |
| **Deposit refund if the visa is refused** | There is **no national rule** — it depends on each university's contract. Always keep the written refusal: universities that refund ask for it | — |

**Bottom line:** the cheapest thing that reliably works is the **place-reservation / enrolment
deposit**, not just an application fee. So the best deals are universities with a **small deposit
that is refunded if the visa is refused.** Always check your own consulate's checklist on
exteriores.gob.es before paying.

## 5. Before you pay anything — send this email

> Subject: Non-EU applicant — visa letter and deposit refund for [programme], September 2027
>
> Hello, I am a non-EU applicant for [programme] starting September/October 2027. Could you
> please confirm:
> 1. After admission, what is the **minimum amount** I must pay to receive the letter for the
>    Spanish student visa?
> 2. Does that letter state that the programme is **full-time and in person**, with the **start and
>    end dates**, the **weekly teaching hours** and the **amount paid**?
> 3. If my visa is **refused**, is that payment refunded — fully or partly — and what documents do
>    you need?
> 4. How many weekly contact hours does the programme have?
> 5. Is it a *Máster Universitario* (official) or a *título propio*?
>
> Thank you.
