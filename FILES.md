# 6 · How the research was made

`.masters-search/` — the toolchain. Open these only to re-run or extend the search.

### The two files that do the real thinking

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| **`qual_level.py`** | **Separates real master's degrees from certificates.** Handles negation, so *"a genuine second-cycle degree, **not** a Lehrgang"* reads correctly instead of backwards. Knows *máster propio*, *título propio*, *Diploma Accademico di II livello* vs *Master di I livello*, RNCP levels, *Universitätslehrgang*. |
| **`corrections.py`** | **Lets a verifier's correction override a stale field.** 75 of 127 verified Door-3 records say a machine field is wrong. This catches the dangerous ones — the Babelsberg and FAMU **live piano auditions** hiding behind "Exam/interview only", Edinburgh's *under €1.5k* that is really **£29,900**. |

# Two things worth knowing about all of this

**"Never checked" is not "fine".** 249 of the 398 Door-3 records were never read by a second agent. In the app that state has no colour of its own — it is an empty meter slot — precisely so it can never be mistaken for good news.

**A correction outranks a field.** Where a verifier's prose contradicts a structured value, the prose wins and you get shown the sentence. Edinburgh's recorded band says *under €1.5k/yr*; the real figure is **≈£29,900**. KASK's says the same; it is **≈€8,800**. Both render struck through and marked wrong, and neither can reach a "cheap" filter.
