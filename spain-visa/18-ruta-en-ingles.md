# 18 — The English-taught route dissolves the problem

## The finding

**If the master's is taught in English, no Spanish certificate is required at all** — verified
directly at UC3M and UPC FIB. Which means:

- no B2 problem,
- **no need for a conditional admission**,
- an **ordinary, unconditional admission letter** — a far stronger document than anything
  conditional.

It also replaces an **8–12 month** problem (Spanish to certified B2 from zero, uncertain end date)
with a **3–6 week** one (an IELTS booking). He controls the timeline instead of hoping.

---

## ❌ First, drop the February idea

**February intakes do not exist in computing or economics at Spanish public universities.** The
only verified ones are at UPC: *Enginyeria Industrial* (ETSEIB) and two civil/structural masters
at Camins — which states they are **the only two** at that school taking second-semester entry.
Both are Catalan/Spanish-taught engineering, not his field.

⚠️ **Beware a common false positive:** many pages that look like a February intake are the second
*matriculación* window for programmes that still start in September (UNED, UCM, UAM).

*(One genuinely interesting detail: UPC Camins publishes its February-2027 decisions in
**December 2026 / January 2027** — exactly his target timing. Wrong field, but it proves the
shape is possible.)*

## 🔑 The real blocker moves: IELTS

**A letter from his Tunisian university will not work.** UC3M waives the requirement only for
native speakers or a degree taught **entirely** in English. In Catalonia the accepted certificates
are a **closed list fixed by law** (Resolució REU/2197/2024) — an institutional letter is not on
it. UPF/BSE: *"if your degree was not in English, you should make arrangements to take an English
language test."*

> **IELTS Academic 6.5 unlocks nearly everything on this list at once** — UC3M (needs 5.5–6.5), the
> Catalan B2 requirement, and BSE's higher bar (TOEFL 90 / IELTS 6.5) simultaneously.
> **Available in Tunis, results in ~13 days.** It is the single highest-leverage action available.

**The one exception worth an email:** **Alicante's Economics with Data Science** accepts B2 *or*
60 ECTS studied in English *or* English-speaking nationality — and its admission checklist marks
the English certificate as **"optional"**, weighting a motivation letter, CV and two references
instead. It also **explicitly welcomes engineering backgrounds.** That is the one place a
discretionary admission without a test looks plausible. Ask them.

---

## ⏰ Who can produce a document by January 2027?

**Only one: UPF / Barcelona School of Economics.** Applications open **autumn 2026**, admission is
**rolling with decisions in 4–6 weeks**, so applying in October–November 2026 plausibly yields an
official admission letter in **November 2026 – January 2027**.

Everyone else structurally cannot: applications *open* in December–February, so no decision
precedes that. UC3M lands **~27 February**, UAB **~16 March**, UPC FIB **30 April**.

⚠️ **But weigh BSE carefully.** At €16,500–18,500, an admission letter from it sits awkwardly beside
a visa file evidencing ~€7,200 — it invites the question of how the master's will be funded. UAB at
~€4,500 raises no such question. **If the goal is a coherent file rather than the earliest possible
paper, UC3M in February or UAB in March is the better document.**

**The fork:** if no English certificate is obtainable, this whole route closes everywhere except
possibly Alicante, and the Spanish-conditional route becomes unavoidable. **So the IELTS decision
is the real decision, and it should be made now.**
