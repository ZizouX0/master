# 05 — The short version: an email exchange as proof of intent

You don't need to be admitted. You need a university to **write back**, in a way that shows
a consular officer this master's is a real plan and not a sentence in a letter. This file is
the fastest route to that: one short email, sent wide, and a way of filing the replies so
they read as evidence.

> Template [`01`](./01-email-preadmision-master.md) is the thorough version — five numbered
> questions, for when you are genuinely applying. **Use this one instead if all you want is
> the exchange.** Long emails to admissions offices go unanswered; short ones get replies.

---

## The rule that decides whether you get a reply

**Ask one thing a busy person can answer in thirty seconds** — and phrase it so the natural
answer is affirmative and quotable.

| Ask this | Because the reply says… |
|---|---|
| *"¿Mi titulación de Ingeniería Informática cumple el perfil de acceso?"* | **"Sí"** — a university on the record confirming you can enter |
| *"¿Cuándo abre la preinscripción para el curso 2027/28?"* | A date — which proves the plan has a calendar, not a wish |

Two questions. Not five. Everything else can wait for the second email, and by then you have
a live thread, which is what you were after in the first place.

---

## The email

**Subject:** `Perfil de acceso — Máster en [NOMBRE], curso 2027/28`

```
Estimados señores:

Soy [NOMBRE Y APELLIDOS], ciudadano tunecino y titulado en Ingeniería Informática por
[UNIVERSIDAD] ([AÑO]). Me interesa cursar su [NOMBRE DEL MÁSTER] en el curso 2027/28.

Antes de iniciar el proceso quisiera confirmar dos puntos:

1. ¿Mi titulación de Ingeniería Informática cumple el perfil de acceso del programa?
2. ¿En qué fechas se abre el plazo de preinscripción para el curso 2027/28?

Estoy preparando mi traslado a España y cursaré previamente un año de español presencial
para alcanzar el nivel exigido, por lo que me sería de gran ayuda conocer el calendario
con antelación.

Muchas gracias por su tiempo.

Un cordial saludo,
[NOMBRE Y APELLIDOS]
[Teléfono con +216] · [correo]
```

That third paragraph is doing quiet work: it tells them why you are asking two years early,
so the question doesn't look strange — and it puts **your own two-step plan into the thread**,
in writing, before they reply. Their answer then sits underneath it.

### English (Berklee Valencia and English-taught programmes)

```
Dear Admissions Team,

I am [FULL NAME], a Tunisian national and a Software Engineering graduate from
[UNIVERSITY] ([YEAR]). I am interested in [PROGRAMME NAME] for the 2027/28 intake.

Two quick questions before I start the process:

1. Does a Software Engineering degree meet the entry profile for this programme?
2. When does the application window for the 2027/28 intake open?

I am relocating to Spain and will first complete a full-time Spanish language year to
reach the required level, so knowing the calendar in advance would help me a great deal.

Thank you for your time.

Kind regards,
[FULL NAME]
[+216 phone] · [email]
```

### The follow-up, if nothing comes back in 10 days

Reply **inside your own thread** — never start a new one. The bump preserves the original
message underneath, which is what you will print.

```
Estimados señores:

Reenvío mi consulta anterior por si no llegó a su destinatario. Bastaría con una respuesta
breve a las dos preguntas. Muchas gracias.
```

**A university carries more weight than an academy.** The rows above are universities or
university-attached centres — a reply on `@ucam.edu` or `@salleurl.edu` reads as
institutional. A reply from a private audio academy reads as a sales response, because that
is usually what it is.

> ⚠️ **Do not name UCAM or La Salle as the *destination* of your itinerary** in the letter
> you sign ([`03`](./03-plan-de-estudios.md)) without checking the contact-hours problem in
> the [README](./README.md) first. As correspondence evidence they are fine. As the plan's
> endpoint, one is hybrid and the other teaches 18:00–20:00, and neither can carry the second
> permission.

## Filing the replies so they count as evidence

A thread only works as evidence if it is obviously real. Print each exchange as **PDF from
the email client, not a screenshot**: full headers, sender address, date, your original
message quoted underneath. Both directions in one file, oldest at the top.

Then put a cover sheet in front of them:

```
                   ÍNDICE DE CORRESPONDENCIA CON CENTROS UNIVERSITARIOS

Solicitante: [NOMBRE Y APELLIDOS] — pasaporte nº [Nº]

  1.  [UNIVERSIDAD]
      Programa: [NOMBRE DEL MÁSTER], curso 2027/28
      Consulta remitida el [FECHA] a [dirección]
      Respuesta recibida el [FECHA] de [nombre y cargo], [dirección]
      Contenido: confirma que la titulación de Ingeniería Informática cumple el
      perfil de acceso e indica la apertura de la preinscripción en [FECHA].
      (Anexo [n], folios [x]–[y].)

  2.  [UNIVERSIDAD] ...

Los presentes documentos se aportan como acreditación del itinerario formativo descrito
en la carta de motivación, sin que constituyan carta de admisión ni matrícula.

                                                Fdo.: [NOMBRE Y APELLIDOS]
```

**That last sentence matters.** It says out loud that these are enquiries, not an admission.
It costs you nothing — the officer can see what they are anyway — and it makes the whole file
read as honest. A dossier that overstates one document gets every other document re-read
with suspicion.

## Two things worth being straight about

**This is the weakest tier of evidence, and that is fine** — because it is not what earns the
visa. The visa is earned by the language school's *carta de admisión* ([`02`](./02-email-escuela-idiomas.md)).
The correspondence answers the follow-up question, *"and after the year of Spanish?"* For
that job it is enough, and it costs you a fortnight and no money.

**If a university offers a free online preinscripción, do it.** Several Spanish universities
let you register an application without paying anything, and the portal emits a
*justificante* PDF with a file number. That single document outweighs any number of email
threads, because it carries a reference the consulate can verify. Ask in your second email:
*"¿Es posible formalizar la preinscripción de forma anticipada y obtener el justificante?"*
