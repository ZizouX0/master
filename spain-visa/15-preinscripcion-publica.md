# 15 — Free preinscripción at a public university: the answer is no, for now

**Can a non-EU applicant today complete a free preinscripción at a Spanish public university and
download a PDF receipt with his name on it?**

> **No.** Every portal open right now either charges a fee that voids the application if unpaid,
> or is closed to you.

Three things converge on that: the 2026/27 cycle has essentially run out, most windows having
closed between June and 1 September; the portals still open gate a non-EHEA applicant behind an
equivalence fee or a Rector's authorisation whose window has passed; and the genuinely free
systems are between phases.

| System | Fee at submission | Named PDF receipt? | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Distrito Único Andaluz** (10 universities) | **None** ✅ | **Yes** — *resguardo* by email + SMS, carrying a *Número de Registro* | Between phases |
| **Valencian** (UPV, UV, UJI, UMH) | **€155.22** — unpaid ⇒ application cancelled | Yes at UV | Closed |
| **Catalan** (UPC, UAB, UdG) | **€30.21** each, non-refundable | Not confirmed | Closed |
| **UCLM** | **€49.40** for non-EHEA | Not stated | Open to 11 Sep |
| **UAM** Madrid | **€27.54** per application | On payment | Closed 1 Sep |
| **UNED** | — | — | Needs a *Autorización del Rector*; that window closed in May |

## ✅ The one free system, and how to use it properly

**The Distrito Único Andaluz is genuinely free**, and it produces exactly what you described: a
PDF *resguardo de solicitud* with your name and a registration number, emailed and texted to you.
**Your passport is accepted as the ID number**, and **one application covers up to six master's
programmes** across all ten Andalusian public universities.

It runs in three phases:

| Phase | Dates (2026/27 cycle) | Who |
|---|---|---|
| **Fase 1** | 13 Jan – 14 Apr | **Exclusively foreign students** with a foreign degree giving master's access at home |
| Fase 2 | 17–26 Jun | Everyone |
| Fase 3 | **8–15 September** | Everyone, vacancies only |

### 🎯 Your target is Fase 1, around January 2027

It is **free**, it produces the named receipt, and it is the phase **built for exactly your
profile** — a foreign, non-homologated degree. It gives a full admission cycle and a realistic
visa runway. Put it in your calendar now.

### ⚠️ And skip Fase 3, even though it opens in days

Fase 3 is open right now-ish and it is tempting. **Don't.** It offers only programmes with places
left over, for a course **starting this month** — so the receipt it generates would say you
applied for **2026/27**. That directly contradicts a study plan built around a language year
followed by a 2027/28 master's. A document that contradicts your own plan is worse than no
document: it invites the officer to ask which version is true.

Apply when the application matches what you actually intend to do.

## Two corrections this produced

**The €400 is not a preinscripción fee.** I described it earlier as the cost of holding a Fase 1
place. More precisely: the **DUA preinscripción itself is free**, and the €400 *prerreserva* is
what unlocks the **carta de admisión** afterwards. Sevilla's own wording: *"una vez hayas hecho la
prerreserva de 400 euros de tu plaza o la matrícula oficial, puedes solicitarla."* So you can
apply, and receive the receipt, without paying anything at all.

**"Free preinscripción" is false in practice in the Valencian system.** The €155.22 equivalence
study is technically separate from preinscripción, but at both UPV and UV an unpaid application is
**cancelled or ineligible for evaluation**. Treat it as a compulsory fee.

---

## The limitation to be clear-eyed about

**A *resguardo* is a submission receipt, not an admission document.** It proves you applied. The
consulate's checklist asks for a *carta de admisión*, which in Andalusia is only issued after the
€400 prerreserva or full enrolment.

So the free receipt is **genuine, verifiable evidence of intent** — with a registration number a
consulate can check, which is more than any email thread gives you. But it does not substitute for
an admission letter, and you should not present it as one.
