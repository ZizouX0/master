# 19 — Conditional admission at public universities: mostly a mirage, and what works instead

## ✏️ A correction

I said **La Laguna (ULL)** and **Almería (UAL)** offer admission conditioned on **pending Spanish**.
**That is largely wrong.** Both — and every other university checked — are implementing the same
national provision, **art. 18.4 of RD 822/2021**, which is for students **finishing a Spanish/EHEA
bachelor's** who still owe their TFG, ≤9 ECTS, and the **foreign-language** credential (usually
English B1) for *that* degree.

UAL says it plainly: the pending item is *"la acreditación de la competencia lingüística en **idioma
extranjero** requerida, el TFG y un máximo de 9 créditos ECTS."*

**It is not a route for a foreign graduate who lacks Spanish.** La Rioja, UCLM, UPO, URJC, UPV and
UPC all replicate the same clause. UPO adds that such applicants *"solamente podrán participar en la
tercera fase."*

**ULL is a partial exception** — its clause is standalone, not limited to foreign language:
*"aunque la persona solicitante todavía no haya finalizado el grado **o no disponga aún de la
acreditación de idioma**."* But conditional places are explicitly **lower priority** and granted
only *"siempre que hayan plazas disponibles."*

---

## 🔑 What actually solves his problem: B1 + an in-house level test

Better than conditional admission, and hiding in plain sight. Several public universities require
only **Spanish B1**, and let an **internal test replace the DELE/SIELE certificate**:

| University | Level | The mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐ **USC** Santiago | **B1** | *"si no posee este título, la USC le realizará una **prueba de conocimiento** de castellano o gallego **antes del inicio de las clases**"* — a test taken right before term starts, i.e. **after his language year**. Functionally identical to conditional admission, without needing one |
| **ULL** La Laguna | **B1** | Certificate *or* the Servicio de Idiomas level test |
| **UAL** Almería | **B1** (certain masters) | Same structure |
| **URJC** | — | *"la Dirección del Máster podrá solicitar la superación de una **prueba de nivel** hablado y escrito"* in lieu of the certificate |
| **UPNA** | — | Test via its Centro Superior de Idiomas |

**This is the answer.** He doesn't need a conditional admission — he needs a university where the
bar is **B1 rather than B2** and where **his own language course satisfies it via an internal
test**. That removes both the certificate deadline and the conditionality.

## ✅ One real document, verified

**UAL issues a *carta de preadmisión* on request** — send passport + degree copy to *Servicios
Administrativos de Alumnos – Área de Acceso*. A *carta de aceptación* follows after enrolment.
That is a genuine, named, obtainable document, and the only one of its kind found in the public
system.

**And UPV's Fase 0 is purpose-built for this exact situation.** Its own PDF:

> *"dirigida a las personas tituladas extranjeras sin nacionalidad europea y sin residencia legal
> en la Unión Europea, con objeto de que dispongan de su **admisión en firme con la antelación
> suficiente para tramitar visados**"* — and it generates a ***certificado de admisión*** which
> *"**sirve habitualmente para la tramitación del visado**."*

⚠️ But UPV warns it may not resolve early: *"es posible que tu solicitud no sea resuelta con
antelación a las fases del periodo ordinario."*

## ⏰ Correction on the January target

**No public university produces a decision in January 2027 for a September 2027 start.**

- **DUA Fase 1:** applications 13–29 January, but the **first adjudication is 19 February**, then
  12 March, 9 April. So a *resguardo* in January — **a decision in February at the earliest.**
- **UC3M:** *"RESOLUCIONES A PARTIR DEL 27 FEBRERO."*
- **UPM:** list 20 March · **URV:** 10–24 April · **ULL:** May.

The only candidates for a January document are **UPV Fase 0** (opens ~17 Nov, not guaranteed early)
and **UPF/BSE** (rolling, 4–6 weeks).

## 🚨 The caveat that undercuts the whole strategy

The consulate checklist (Casablanca, Jan 2025 — nearest published analogue to Tunis) requires the
admission letter to state ***"asignaturas en las que se ha matriculado"*** and, in capitals:

> **"PAGO DE LA MATRÍCULA. SIN EL PAGO DE LA MATRÍCULA NO SE RECOGERÁ EL VISADO."**

**A conditional letter with no enrolment behind it will very likely not produce a visa.** Which
means conditional admission cannot be the *basis* of the visa — only supporting evidence alongside
the paid language-course enrolment. **Verify with the Tunis embassy before building any plan on
it.**

---

## So, the three routes that actually work

1. **English-taught masters** — **UC3M** (Economics, Finance, MBA, Management, Big Data Analytics;
   its *Business and Finance* entry profile **explicitly names Engineering**), **UPF**, **UPV
   *Gestión de Empresas, Productos y Servicios*** (B2 English, no Spanish). No Spanish requirement
   at all, so conditionality disappears. Decision late Feb 2027.
2. **UPV Fase 0**, from ~17 Nov 2026 — the only mechanism explicitly designed to issue an early,
   visa-usable *certificado de admisión*. Pair it with one of its English-requirement masters.
3. **Spanish-taught, B1 + level test** — **USC**, **ULL**, **UAL**. The bar is B1, the test is
   taken in Spain before classes begin, and his language year satisfies it directly.

⚠️ Note UPV charges **double public fees** to non-resident non-EU students (Decreto 101/2024).
