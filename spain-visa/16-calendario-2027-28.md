# 16 — The 2027/28 calendar, and the free routes to a named PDF

## First, the honest state of play

**No Spanish public university has published a 2027/28 master's calendar yet.** Every one checked
— the Andalusian DUA, UPV, UC3M, UCM, UAM, UPNA, UDC, Murcia, UPC — still shows 2026/27 only.
So every 2027/28 date below is **inferred from the same-week-each-year pattern**, not published.

---

## ✏️ A correction: the Andalusian Fase 1 is 17 days, not three months

I told you Fase 1 ran "~13 Jan – 14 Apr". **Wrong.** The 2026/27 Fase 1 ran

> **13–29 January 2026** — seventeen days.

The April dates I quoted are the *matrícula* deadline, not the application window. **If you miss
those seventeen days you wait a year.** That makes it something to diarise precisely, not
approximately.

## 💰 Free **and** returns a named PDF: only two systems

| | Fee | Named PDF |
|---|---|---|
| **Distrito Único Andaluz** | **€0** | ✅ BOJA: *"el sistema informático facilitará una copia de ésta como justificante de su presentación"* |
| **UCM Madrid** | **€0** for *másteres oficiales* — the €40 you may read about applies to *títulos propios* | ✅ downloadable *resguardo de preinscripción* |

Everything else charges: UC3M and UAM €27.54, Catalonia €30.21, UPV €155.22, UCLM €49.40.

**So your two free routes to a named document are the DUA in mid-January and UCM in late January.**
Both are free, both produce a downloadable receipt with your name, and the DUA covers **ten
universities in one application**.

## 🆕 Two things worth knowing

**UAM runs an Early Admission reserved for international students, opening 1 October** — about four
weeks away, and the earliest public opportunity of the whole cycle. Its stated purpose is visa and
scholarship lead time. Two caveats: the engineering GPA threshold is **7.30/10**, and it is
submitted **by email** rather than a portal, so there is **no system-generated receipt** — plus
€27.54 and a €300 deposit on admission. Worth checking your average against that threshold.

**Galicia runs a *convocatoria anticipada* for foreign-degree holders** at USC, UDC and UVigo. And
a detail that matters if Galicia interests you: **at UDC, a non-EHEA degree holder can ONLY enter
through that early call** — places capped at 50% of the programme. Miss it and the ordinary round
is closed to you.

## And a definitive answer to a question worth closing

> **Is there any Spanish public university with year-round or rolling master's admission?**
> **No.** Every public institution uses fixed windows with batch resolutions on published dates.
> Rolling admission in Spain is a private-sector practice.

The closest is **UC3M**, whose three back-to-back periods run 1 December to 31 May with no gap —
a six-month season, not year-round, and €27.54 per master.

---

## Where the DUA dates will appear, and when to look

Fase 1 is governed by its **own separate Acuerdo** of the Comisión del Distrito Único, distinct
from the main admission resolution. For 2026/27 that Acuerdo was dated **12 December 2025** and
published in **BOJA nº 245 of 22 December 2025**.

**So watch BOJA and the DUA calendar page in mid-December 2026** — not in spring, when the main
resolution appears. Applications then open around mid-January 2027 at
[lajunta.es/portaldua](https://lajunta.es/portaldua).
