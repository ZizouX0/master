Two different questions. The four easy-access universities score well on the first and all
score the same on the second.

## Are they master's degrees? Not officially — and you should know exactly what that means

**All four are *títulos propios*.** In Spain the word *"máster"* in a programme name does not
mean a state-recognised degree. There are two parallel things with the same word on the tin:

| | **Máster Universitario Oficial** | **Título propio** *(what all four are)* |
|---|---|---|
| Issued by | The Ministry of Education | The university, under its own authority |
| In the RUCT register | Yes | **No** |
| ANECA-verified | Yes | No |
| Gives PhD access | Yes | **No** |
| Recognised across the EHEA | Yes | Not automatically |
| **Valid for a Spanish student visa** | Yes | **Yes** — RD 1155/2024 accepts both |

So they *are* genuine 60-ECTS postgraduate qualifications from genuine universities — ENTI's
carries the **Universitat de Barcelona** name — and they do not endanger your visa. What you
lose is standing: no doctorate later, and recognition back in Tunisia is a question you should
ask the Tunisian ministry **before** you pay, not after. The full treatment is in
[`results/DEGREE-CHECK.md`](../results/DEGREE-CHECK.md) — 180 of the 1,635 programmes in your
search are look-alikes of this kind.

For a producer this may genuinely not matter: nobody in a studio asks whether your master's is
in the RUCT. For anything academic, it matters completely.

## The two that *are* official degrees

### La Salle – Universitat Ramon Llull — *Máster Universitario en Acústica, Audio y Vibraciones*

The strongest degree in the whole Spanish slice: **official, ministry-issued, 60 ECTS, taught
entirely in English, €11,940, 30 places**, no portfolio, no audition, rolling admission with
**a decision within 8 days of a complete application**. And its entry profile is inverted from
every other programme here — **it wants engineers and does not want musicians.**

⚠️ **But check this before you get attached.** The verified requirements page publishes a
**closed list** of accepted bachelor's degrees:

> Mechanical Engineering · Telecommunications Systems Engineering · Industrial Technology
> Engineering · Audiovisual Systems · Physics · Aerospace Vehicle Engineering · Mathematics ·
> Civil Engineering

**Software Engineering / Informática is not on it.** An earlier note in your data read the
wording as "or similar" and was corrected — the list is closed as published. Whether a Tunisian
*Ingénierie Informatique* is accepted as equivalent to Telecommunications Systems Engineering
is **exactly the question to put to them in an email**, and it is a good one: specific,
answerable, and it produces a written reply either way.

Also remember the timetable problem from the [README](./README.md) — La Salle teaches evenings.
Even if they accept your degree, confirm the weekly presencial hours before building the plan
on it.

### Berklee Valencia — *MM in Music Production, Technology and Innovation*

**Dual status, verified:** a US Master of Music from Berklee (NECHE-accredited) *and* an
official Spanish master's delivered under affiliation with the **Universitat Politècnica de
València**, with an ANECA verified report. Full-time, presencial, visa-safe, your subject
exactly.

The price is ≈ USD 50,430 and the door is a real one: portfolio, IELTS 7.5, a $150 application
fee. Not part of the two-week email plan — but it is the answer if someone asks whether a real,
official master's in your field exists in Spain.
