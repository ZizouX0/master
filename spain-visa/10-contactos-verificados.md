# 10 — Verified contacts

Every address below was read on the university's **own domain**. Where no address is published,
this file says so and gives the contact page instead — **nothing here is guessed**. If you find
an address in this file that turns out to be wrong, the fix is to open the linked page, never to
invent a variation.

> 🗓️ **Nothing for 2027/28 is published yet by anyone.** All calendars below are the 2026/27
> cycle. That is the right precedent to cite, and it changes how you ask: don't request "the
> 2027/28 dates", ask for the **expected** period. See the revised question at the bottom.

### 🥇 Write to UPC first

`informacio@fib.upc.edu` reaches **three official English-taught programmes at once**, and FIB
is **the only school in this whole search that publishes its weekly contact hours** —
*"20h lectures per week"*, in writing, on a public page. That single sentence is the strongest
piece of visa evidence available to you, because 20 h/week presencial is exactly the legal test
your permission turns on. Print that page and keep it.

### Four cautions that will cost you a fortnight each

1. **URV runs the same degree online and face-to-face.** An admission letter that does not say
   *presencial* will not support a visa. Ask explicitly:
   *"¿Pueden confirmarme que la admisión sería a la modalidad presencial?"*
2. **URV's address contains a deliberate anti-spam insert.** `(ELIMINAR)` means "delete", so the
   real address is almost certainly `master@urv.cat` — but that is an inference, not something
   published. Open the page and copy the `mailto:` if it fails.
3. **UC3M publishes no email at all.** Submit the form, then ask them to reply **from a
   `uc3m.es` address so you have something printable**. Do not construct
   `postgrado@uc3m.es` — an email that bounces is worse than no email.
4. **UPM's EMSE (software engineering) page is stale** — it still quotes 2013–14 prices and its
   programme site returns 404. The degree is still listed, but confirm it runs before relying
   on it. `master.cd@fi.upm.es` is the better-maintained UPM door.

### Two you cannot email yet

- **UPF Barcelona — Master in Intelligent Interactive Systems.** Confirmed official and
  face-to-face **on the Generalitat de Catalunya's own degree register**, but `upf.edu` sits
  behind a bot challenge that automated tools cannot pass. **Open
  [upf.edu/web/iis](https://www.upf.edu/web/iis/) in a normal browser and take the address from
  the page.**
- **IE University — MCSBT.** The only address findable site-wide is `student.services@ie.edu`,
  which is student services, not admissions. Fees are gated behind a form, and the page hedges
  on whether the official degree is automatic. Low priority.

**FIB will want**, apostilled and sworn-translated: an academic transcript **stating hours and
credits per subject**, plus a diploma certifying **≥3 years** of study and postgraduate access
in Tunisia. Order those now — they are slow, and every university on this list wants the same
things.

### Also worth knowing

- **UC3M's Computer Science and Technology** and **Cybersecurity** masters are **bilingual**,
  not English-only — a minimum Spanish level is required. After your language year, that is an
  advantage, not a barrier. €84.07/ECTS.
- **UPV's AI master (MUIARFID)** is **100% Spanish**, so it's out of the English list — but its
  calendar is the useful precedent: **Phase 0 is reserved for non-EU international applicants
  and opened 17 Nov 2025**, ten months ahead of the intake. Spanish universities open the
  non-EU door early. Plan for that.
- **UPM's EIT Digital masters** are English and official but **split across two countries**,
  which complicates a single-country visa. Skip.

## The revised question to ask

Because no 2027/28 calendar exists yet, replace question 2 in the
[template](./09-lista-de-envio.md) with this — it is answerable today, and both halves are
exactly what a Spanish consulate file needs:

> *"¿Podrían confirmarme el periodo previsto de preinscripción para el curso 2027/28, y si el
> programa seguirá impartiéndose íntegramente de forma presencial, indicando las horas lectivas
> semanales?"*
