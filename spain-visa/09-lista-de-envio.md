# 09 — Send list and tracker

The mail-merge sheet. Contacts get filled in from the research in
[`10-contactos-verificados.md`](./10-contactos-verificados.md); this file is the *discipline*
around sending, because a well-run correspondence file is worth more than a large one.

## Rules that make the thread usable as evidence

1. **One email address, all the way through.** Use a serious one —
   `nombre.apellido@…`, not a nickname. Every reply must land in the same inbox so the threads
   stay whole.
2. **Never start a second thread with the same university.** Follow up *inside* the original,
   so your first message stays quoted underneath their reply. A thread that contains both
   directions is evidence; two separate messages are not.
3. **Save each reply as PDF the day it arrives** — from the mail client, with full headers.
   Not a screenshot. Name the file `AAAA-MM-DD_Universidad_asunto.pdf`.
4. **Send in waves of five**, two days apart. If your first wave gets no replies, the email is
   wrong, and you want to know that after five sends and not after thirty.
5. **Send Tuesday–Thursday, 08:00–10:00 Madrid time.** Monday is triage, Friday is gone.
6. **Never attach anything to the first email.** Attachments from unknown senders go to spam.
   Send the CV only when they ask, or in your second message.

## The email, final — copy and paste

**Subject:** `Acceso a máster con título extranjero — Ingeniería Informática (Túnez), curso 2027/28`

```
Estimados señores:

Soy [NOMBRE Y APELLIDOS], ciudadano tunecino y titulado en Ingeniería Informática por
[UNIVERSIDAD] ([AÑO]). Me interesa cursar el [MÁSTER UNIVERSITARIO EN ...] en el curso
2027/28.

Quisiera confirmar dos puntos antes de iniciar el proceso:

1. ¿Puedo acceder al máster con mi título tunecino sin homologación, mediante el estudio
   de equivalencia de nivel de la universidad? ¿Cuál es la tasa y el plazo de resolución?
2. ¿En qué fechas se abre la preinscripción para el curso 2027/28, y cuántas horas
   lectivas semanales presenciales tiene el programa?

Cursaré previamente un año de español presencial en España para alcanzar el nivel
requerido, por lo que conocer el calendario con antelación me resulta necesario.

Muchas gracias por su tiempo.

Un cordial saludo,
[NOMBRE Y APELLIDOS] · [+216 ...] · [correo]
```

**English, for the English-taught programmes:**

**Subject:** `Master's access with a foreign degree — Computer Engineering (Tunisia), 2027/28 intake`

```
Dear Admissions Team,

I am [FULL NAME], a Tunisian national and a graduate in Computer/Software Engineering from
[UNIVERSITY] ([YEAR]). I am interested in the [PROGRAMME NAME] for the 2027/28 intake.

Two questions before I begin the process:

1. Can I be admitted with my Tunisian degree without homologación, through the
   university's equivalence assessment? What is the fee and the processing time?
2. When does the application period for 2027/28 open, and how many scheduled on-campus
   contact hours per week does the programme have?

I will first complete a full-time, in-person Spanish language year in Spain, so knowing
the calendar in advance is important for my planning.

Thank you for your time.

Kind regards,
[FULL NAME] · [+216 ...] · [email]
```
