# 🇪🇸 Spain — student visa on the "language year → master's" plan

**Your plan:** arrive in Spain on a study visa for an intensive Spanish course, then move
straight into a master's in music production / sound. This folder holds the paperwork that
makes the consulate see **one coherent two-step itinerary** instead of "a language course
with a vague hope attached".

> ⚠️ **Nothing in this folder is a university document, and nothing here should ever be made
> to look like one.** The admission letters have to be issued by the schools themselves.
> Templates 01 and 02 are the emails that get them to issue one; template 03 is the letter
> **you** sign, which is a normal, expected part of the file.

---

## The one thing to understand first

Spain does not grant a study visa for an *intention*. It grants it for **the course you are
enrolled in right now**. Under the current Reglamento (RD 1155/2024) the test is:

| Test | What it means | What fails it |
|---|---|---|
| **Admission** | A signed *carta de admisión* from the centre, for a named course with dates | A quote, a brochure, an "application received" email |
| **20 lectivas hours/week, presencial** | The course must be full-time and in person | Evening courses, part-time, hybrid, online |
| **Duration** | Over 90 days ⇒ national (type D) visa, not Schengen | A 4-week course gets you a tourist visa, not a study one |
| **Means** | ~100% IPREM per month of stay — **IPREM 2026 is frozen at €600/month**, so ≈ €7,200 for a year | A balance that appeared last week |
| **Insurance** | Private, full cover, valid in Spain, **no co-payments, no waiting period** | Travel insurance |

**So the language course is what actually earns the visa.** It has to clear 20 h/week
presencial on its own. The master's evidence does a different job: it answers the question the
consular officer is really asking — *"and then what?"* A file that says "Spanish course, then I
go home with nothing" is weak. A file that says "Spanish course → B2 → this named master's at
this named university, here is their letter" is a **study itinerary**, and that reads as
credible.

---

## The trap that would bite you at step two

You do not need a new visa for the master's — from inside Spain you file a **prórroga de
estancia por estudios** at the Oficina de Extranjería (in the 60 days before your first
permission expires), with the master's admission letter, means and insurance.

**But the prórroga is judged by the same 20 h/week presencial test.** That kills two of the
three Spanish options already in [`results/PRIVATE-SCHOOLS.md`](../results/PRIVATE-SCHOOLS.md):

| Programme | Cost | Verdict for the itinerary |
|---|---|---|
| **UCAM Murcia** — Máster en Producción Musical | €3,900 | ⚠️ **semipresencial (hybrid)**. Cheap and your degree is named in its entry profile, but hybrid is exactly what the contact-hours test rejects. **Do not build the itinerary on it until UCAM confirms the presencial hours in writing** |
| **La Salle – URL Barcelona** | ~€199/ECTS | ❌ timetable is **Mon–Fri 18:00–20:00** = 10 h/week. Legally unusable for the permission |
| **Berklee Valencia** — MPTI | ≈ USD 50,430 | ✅ Full-time, presencial, visa-safe — and 15× UCAM |

👉 **Name in your study plan a master's that itself passes the 20 h/week presencial test.**
If you name a hybrid or evening programme, you are handing the officer a plan that visibly
cannot be completed — and you are also planning your own refusal a year from now.

If none of the three works, the honest and still-strong version of the itinerary is:
*"Spanish to B2 in Spain, then a master's in the EU"* — and you support it with the
pre-admission correspondence you actually have, wherever it is from.

## Where you actually apply

Tunisia's **BLS centre handles Schengen (≤90 days) only** — a study visa over 90 days is a
**national visa filed with the Embassy of Spain in Tunis directly**. The published route for
long-stay applications is to write to **`Emb.tunez@maec.es`** for the appointment and the
current checklist.

> Ask them for **their** checklist in writing before you assemble anything. Consulates vary,
> the list here is the shape of the file, not a substitute for theirs.
