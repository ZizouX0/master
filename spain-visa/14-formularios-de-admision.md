# 14 — Admission forms that give you a named document, free

You fill in a form; the university sends back a document with **your name on it**. Here is where
that actually works, outside Madrid.

> ⏰ **Timing first.** The 2026/27 year is starting now, so **2027/28 is not selectable anywhere
> yet.** Navarra's pattern is that master's admission opens **1 October** for the following
> course. The **rolling portals** below are the ones most likely to take a next-year application
> today — and they are also the free ones.

---

## 🥇 The three worth doing

### 1 · UIC Barcelona — the cleanest document, and it arrives before any money

**Form:** [app.uic.es/crm/admission-type/en](https://app.uic.es/crm/admission-type/en) · free ·
account needed · **decision in ~15 days by email**

Their own wording: *"Once you have been admitted, **we will send you an official letter** so that
you can reserve your place by paying 20% of the total programme fee"* — and accepted candidates
*"will be contacted by post."*

**The letter arrives before you pay anything.** A physical, posted, named letter, and you then
have 15 days to decide about the deposit. You simply don't take that step. This is the cleanest
"document in my name that isn't an invoice" found anywhere.

### 2 · La Salle – Universitat Ramon Llull — fastest, 8 days

**Form:** [admissions.salleurl.edu](https://admissions.salleurl.edu/) · free · **"8 days maximum"**

Minimal documents (passport, degree, CV), and it returns a named **"Pre-registration document"**
by email. Fastest published turnaround of the thirteen universities checked. Its *reserva* is paid
afterwards, and is refundable minus €300 on a visa denial.

### 3 · Universitat Abat Oliba CEU — the only one that names the visa

**Form:** [admision.uaoceu.es](https://admision.uaoceu.es/) · *"proceso de admisión 100% online y
gratuito"* · **7 days**

Their own words:

> *"Si eres un alumno internacional la Universitat Abat Oliba CEU te facilitará la **credencial de
> admisión** por medio de la cual podrás obtener el **visado de estudios**... recibirás tu
> credencial de admisión mediante correo electrónico, un SMS y una carta a tu domicilio."*

⚠️ **But read the sentence before it:** *"Una vez realizados todos los trámites anteriores"* — and
"all the previous steps" plausibly includes the **reserva de plaza of €500–€1,500**. So the
credencial may sit behind that payment. **The application itself is free and decided in 7 days.**
Send one email asking whether the credencial is issued before the reserva, before relying on it.

### Runner-up · CEU Cardenal Herrera, Valencia

[admision.uchceu.es](https://admision.uchceu.es/) · free · lightest document burden of all
(passport + highest academic document). You immediately get **a personal intranet account with
credentials in your name**, plus an emailed decision. No published timeline.

### Also free, and it says the quiet part out loud

**Mondragon** — [inskripzioak.mondragon.edu](https://inskripzioak.mondragon.edu/s/login/?language=en_GB&centro=M)
— explicitly free, and the only university that ties the free step to visas in its own words:

> *"The admission registration process is a **free** process that should be started as soon as
> possible in order to **manage visa** and necessary documentation to access studies in Spain."*

Reserva afterwards is 8% of tuition (≈€874).

## 💸 Not free — know before you click

| University | What it costs at the form stage |
|---|---|
| **UCAM** Murcia | **€235** preinscripción (Spanish) / **€280** (English), **non-refundable**, plus €1,200 reserva. And the admission process *does not start until your documents are legalised* |
| **U. de Navarra** | **€100 non-refundable**, and it is **general to all masters** — not a one-off |
| **UPSA** Salamanca | **€350** at the *solicitud* step (credited against tuition) |
| **UCV** Valencia | Free to start, but internationals are pushed to an adviser; reserva is **20%, non-refundable** |

## 🚩 Two traps

**`ucam.edu/admision` is a brochure form, not an admission form.** It asks *"¿En qué podemos
ayudarte?"* and *"¿Cómo quieres que contactemos contigo?"* — it produces a sales call, not a
document. UCAM's real form is elsewhere, and it costs €235.

**Universidad Europea de Valencia has no self-service admission form at all.** The entire funnel
is *solicita información* → adviser → phone interview → reserva de plaza. Worst fit for your
purpose.

Also: **Universidad Católica de Ávila's form is a `.docx` you fill in and email.** Free and
simple, but there is no portal, no reference number and no automatic acknowledgement — so nothing
verifiable comes back.

## What to actually do

1. **Start with UIC Barcelona and La Salle.** Both free, both return a named document, one in
   8 days and one before any payment is due. Two applications, one evening.
2. **Add Mondragon and CEU Cardenal Herrera** — free, and each produces a personalised record.
3. **Email UAO CEU first** to ask whether the *credencial de admisión* is issued before the
   reserva. If yes, it is the single best document on this list, because it names the visa.
4. **Use your name exactly as it appears on your passport.** A document that does not match is
   worse than none.
5. **Do not pay any reserva.** Every one of these will ask, some within 15 days. You are not
   enrolling; you are collecting a document you are entitled to as an applicant. Decline politely
   and keep the letter.

> **And keep it in proportion.** Even the best of these is still supporting evidence. The visa
> rests on a paid, full-time language-course enrolment — see [`13`](./13-admision-condicional.md)
> for the consulate's own wording on that.
