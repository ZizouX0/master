# 17 — The five verified in depth, and a reversal of my shortlist

I recommended UIC first and UAX second. **A close reading of their own published rules and fee
schedules reverses both.** Here is what a deep verification of all five found.

---

## ⛔ First: no route delivers a 2027/28 document today

The 2026/27 year is starting now; **2027/28 is the next cycle and is not open anywhere.** Every
dated artefact readable at all five is 2026/27 or older — UAX's reserva table is *"CURSO
2026/2027"*, La Salle's price list *"Prices 2026-2027"*, UFV's fee table still *"Curso 2025-26"*,
UIC's *"TASAS UIC 2025-2026"*. **None of the five portals exposes an intake or year selector on
any page reachable without an account.**

La Salle's one public signal: its admissions cycle *"begins on 15 October"* — so **~15 October
2026** is when the 2027/28 door plausibly opens. About six weeks away.

---

## 🥇 UFV — the best route, and it wasn't in my top two

| | |
|---|---|
| **Free?** | ✅ Stated plainly. No *estudio de expediente*, no tramitación, no test fee at postgraduate level |
| **Document** | ✅ A named ***carta de admisión*** |
| **Before payment?** | ✅ Yes — reserva follows admission, **15 días naturales** |
| **Reserva** | ✅ **€30–€120** — by far the smallest of the five, and deducted from tuition |
| **Turnaround** | ✅ **48/72 hours** — the fastest published anywhere |
| **Visa refusal** | ✅ **The only written clause of the five:** *"si se le denegara la concesión del visado, la UFV procederá a devolverle el importe abonado"* |

**Two frictions.** It requires your degree **apostilled and sworn-translated at application** —
*"debidamente legalizado y apostillado, junto con su traducción jurada"* — plus a CV, motivation
letter and **two reference letters**. And the candidate portal is **opened by staff, not
self-service**: *"Complete the online admission form or send an email to postgrado@ufv.es so that
we can register you."*

**So your first move is an email to `postgrado@ufv.es`** asking whether a **curso 2027/28**
application can be opened. That one message settles the year question definitively and starts the
process.

---

## 🥈 Nebrija — the one that doesn't need your apostille

Free, and explicitly *"no te supone compromiso ni coste económico"*. Issues a named **carta de
admisión** (Reglamento Art. 11.2). And it has **the most forgiving paperwork terms of the five**:

> It permits *matrícula condicionada* with **"Documentos pendientes de legalización"**, and
> *"El plazo máximo para la aportación de la documentación definitiva será de tres meses desde el
> inicio de la docencia."*

**No apostille needed to apply.** For someone who has not yet started the Tunisian apostille, that
is worth a great deal.

⚠️ Weaker on: **no published turnaround**, **no published reserva amount**, **no visa-refusal
refund**, and its Reglamento binds applications to *"cada curso académico y programa"*. The intake
question rests on a *"Elige prueba de Admisión"* dropdown that loads dynamically and could not be
read — so ask `informa@nebrija.es`.

---

## 🥉 La Salle — good, but its named document comes *after* you pay

Free for **master's** — and note the **€100 application fee you may read about is undergraduate
only**. It commits to a response *"in a maximum period of 8 days"*, and has a real visa clause:
**refund less €300** on presentation of supporting documentation.

**But I described this wrongly.** The 8-day output is **an acceptance email**. The named
*"Pre-registration document"* is issued **after the place reservation** — and that reservation is
**part of total tuition**, commonly cited at **€1,500**, the largest of the five. Their own words:
*"Acceptance to the program DOES NOT IMPLY the automatic reservation of the place."*

💡 One genuinely useful find: La Salle publishes a **€500 "change of call"** fee to move a start
date. That is a documented mechanism for shifting a 2026/27 admission to a 2027 start — worth
asking `admissions@salleurl.edu` about directly.

---

## ⚠️ UIC Barcelona — I put this first. Don't apply until they confirm it's free.

Its own fee schedule (*TASAS UIC 2025-2026*) carries live line items for **"Pruebas de admisión"**
— reported at **€115** — plus *"Gestión de expedientes internacionales"*. And its international
page tells applicants to upload *"proof of payment of processing fees."*

There is mitigating evidence: the entrance-exam page frames exams as **grado** only, and the
non-refundable equivalence fee is scoped to **doctorate** applicants. **But it could not be proven
that master's applicants are exempt.**

Two further problems: **its two official pages contradict each other** on whether apostille and
sworn translation are needed at application or at enrolment — material for you specifically. And
there is **no visa-refusal refund**.

The acceptance letter itself is still good — *"the university will send you an official letter"*,
**before** the 20% reservation. But **email `admisiones@uic.es` first** and get written
confirmation that a master's application costs nothing.

---

## 🛑 UAX — avoid outright. Its own rule defeats the purpose.

From the official *Guía de Admisión de Postgrado*, verbatim:

> ***"La admisión solamente será válida para el curso académico en el que se realiza el proceso de
> admisión, y quedará supeditada a la realización del pago de la matrícula."***

Admission is valid **only for the year you apply in**, and is **subordinated to paying enrolment**
— not a reserva, the *matrícula*. On admission you get **3 to 5 calendar days** to pay. Apostille
is required at application. There is no visa-refusal refund.

And the form itself was read at the source: its JavaScript bundle contains **no "curso", no
"convocatoria", no academic-year field at all**. It is a lead-capture form. **No *carta de
admisión* is issued as the normal output** — that letter appears only in the *traslado de
expediente* procedure, downstream.

**I ranked this second. It should not be on the list.**

## What to actually do this week

Send **one email each** to `postgrado@ufv.es`, `informa@nebrija.es` and `admissions@salleurl.edu`,
asking the same thing: **can I lodge an application for curso 2027/28, and when does that cycle
open?** Add for La Salle: *"¿Es posible solicitar un cambio de convocatoria a 2027/28?"*

Those three replies cost nothing, settle the year question, and are themselves dated named
correspondence from Spanish universities about your 2027/28 entry — which is the evidence you
wanted in the first place.

**And start the apostille and the sworn translation of your degree now.** Four to eight weeks, it
blocks UFV and most public routes, and it is entirely within your control.

# The final answer: the CEU family, and one clear winner

A last sweep of the church-affiliated universities and *centros adscritos* turned up a better
option than anything above.

## 🏆 CEU Cardenal Herrera (Valencia) — the best route found anywhere

**Form:** [admision.uchceu.es](https://admision.uchceu.es/) · **free**

It is the only institution whose own page states in plain words that **the named document precedes
payment**:

> *"you will receive an **admission letter including the instructions for the place reservation**"*
> — and *"download your admission letter once you have been accepted."*

And it has **the lightest document requirement of every institution checked in this whole
project**: at submission, only

> *"Fotocopia del DNI, pasaporte o equivalente"* + *"Fotocopia del documento académico de mayor
> nivel."*

**No apostille. No sworn translation. No reference letters. No motivation letter.** For someone who
has not yet begun the Tunisian apostille, that removes the single biggest obstacle in this file.

**Contacts:** `international@uchceu.es` · `admission@uchceu.es` · `admission.en@uchceu.es`

⚠️ **One caveat:** that sequencing sentence is quoted from the international **undergraduate** page.
The postgraduate page describes the same structure but doesn't repeat it. **Confirm by email
first** — one line, and it settles it.

---

## ✅ The Abat Oliba question, settled

The phrase that worried me — *"una vez realizados todos los trámites anteriores"* — **is on the
undergraduate page**, attached to step 8 of 8, where step 6 is the €1,500 reserva. **And it
attaches to the wrong document.** UAO CEU issues three:

| Document | When | Free? |
|---|---|---|
| **Carta de admisión** — the Comisión's decision, ~7 days | ✅ **Before payment** | ✅ Free |
| **Credencial de admisión** — a *matriculation* credential, not a visa letter | ❌ After the €500–1,500 reserva | ❌ |
| **"Certificado de Admisión"** — the visa-grade one | ❓ Ambiguous — treat as post-payment | ❓ |

The decisive evidence: UAO's own Early Enrolment scholarship requires applicants to submit a
*"carta de admisión en la Universitat"* and **only then** commits them to *"realizar la reserva de
plaza en un plazo no superior a tres semanas."* So the carta is in your hands before any money.

**Master's admission at UAO is explicitly *"100 % online y gratuito"*, decided in seven days.**
Ask `masters@uao.es` one question: *can the carta de admisión be used for the visa without paying
the reserva?*
