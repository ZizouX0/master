# 11 — The universal email, and the lines you bolt on

Eighteen bespoke letters were right for eighteen priority targets. For a list of forty, write
**one** email well and add one or two lines per university. Same effort, five times the reach.

---

## The universal email — Spanish

**Asunto:** `Acceso a máster con título extranjero — Ingeniería Informática (Túnez), curso 2027/28`

```
Estimados señores:

Soy [NOMBRE Y APELLIDOS], ciudadano tunecino y titulado en Ingeniería Informática por
[UNIVERSIDAD] ([AÑO]). Me interesa cursar el [NOMBRE EXACTO DEL MÁSTER] en el curso
2027/28.

Quisiera confirmar cuatro puntos antes de iniciar el proceso:

1. ¿Puedo acceder al máster con mi título tunecino sin homologación, mediante el estudio
   de equivalencia de nivel? ¿Cuál es la tasa y el plazo de resolución, y qué documentos
   debo aportar apostillados y con traducción jurada?
2. ¿Qué periodo de preinscripción está previsto para el curso 2027/28?
3. ¿Cuántas horas lectivas semanales presenciales tiene el programa y cuál es su horario?
4. ¿Emite la universidad carta de admisión válida para solicitar el visado nacional de
   estudios ante la Embajada de España en Túnez?

Cursaré previamente un año de español presencial en España para alcanzar el nivel
requerido, por lo que necesito conocer el calendario con antelación.

Muchas gracias por su tiempo.

Un cordial saludo,
[NOMBRE Y APELLIDOS] · [+216 TELÉFONO] · [CORREO]
```

## The universal email — English

**Subject:** `Master's access with a foreign degree — Computer Engineering (Tunisia), 2027/28 intake`

```
Dear Sir or Madam,

I am [FULL NAME], a Tunisian national and a graduate in Computer Engineering from
[UNIVERSITY] ([YEAR]). I am interested in the [EXACT PROGRAMME NAME] for the 2027/28
intake.

Four questions before I begin the process:

1. Can I be admitted with my Tunisian degree without homologación, through the
   university's level-equivalence assessment? What is the fee and processing time, and
   which documents must be apostilled and sworn-translated?
2. What is the expected application period for the 2027/28 intake?
3. How many scheduled on-campus contact hours per week does the programme have, and what
   is the timetable?
4. Does the university issue an admission letter valid for a Spanish national student
   visa application?

I will first complete a full-time, in-person Spanish language year in Spain, so knowing
the calendar in advance matters for my planning.

Thank you for your time.

Kind regards,
[FULL NAME] · [+216 PHONE] · [EMAIL]
```

### Why these four questions and not others

Each one does a job in the visa file, and each is answerable in under a minute — which is why
they get answered at all.

| Question | What the answer gives you |
|---|---|
| 1 — equivalence | Opens the **paid, referenced administrative file** that is far better evidence than any email |
| 2 — calendar | Dates. A plan with dates is a plan; without them it is a wish |
| 3 — **contact hours** | The number your permission legally turns on. **Never omit this one** |
| 4 — admission letter | Tells you now whether this university can ever produce the document you need |

## The bolt-on lines

Add only what applies. One extra sentence, never a fifth paragraph.

| If the university is… | Add this line |
|---|---|
| **In Andalucía** (Málaga, Granada, Sevilla, Jaén, Almería, Córdoba, Huelva, Cádiz) | *"Entiendo que la admisión se tramita por el Distrito Único Andaluz y que la Fase 1 está reservada a titulaciones extranjeras no homologadas: ¿podrían confirmarme sus fechas para 2027/28 y si la prematrícula de 400 € se reembolsa en caso de denegación del visado?"* |
| **UPV (Valencia) — and only UPV** ⚠️ | *"¿Existe una Fase 0 de preinscripción reservada al estudiantado internacional no comunitario, y cuáles serían sus fechas?"* — **Fase 0 is unique to UPV.** Every other Valencian university opens one general phase for everyone (UV 30 Mar, UJI 15 Apr, UMH 16 Mar), so do not ask them about it. See [`10`](./10-contactos-verificados.md) |
| **Offering the same degree online too** | *"¿Pueden confirmarme que la admisión sería a la modalidad presencial y que así constaría en la carta de admisión?"* |
| **Private** | *"¿Se trata de un Máster Universitario oficial? ¿Podrían indicarme su código RUCT?"* |
| **Evening or weekend timetable** | *"¿Cuántas horas lectivas semanales presenciales computa oficialmente el programa? Mi permiso de estancia por estudios exige un mínimo de 20."* |
| **Not publishing its price** | *"¿Cuál es el importe total de la matrícula para un estudiante extracomunitario en 2027/28?"* |
| **Writing to a named coordinator** | Open with *"Estimado profesor:"* and add *"¿Considera que mi formación previa encaja con el perfil de acceso, y prevé complementos formativos en mi caso?"* |

## Sending forty of these without it becoming a mess

1. **Write the base email once**, save it as a draft, and duplicate it. Change only the
   programme name, the greeting and the bolt-on line.
2. **Send in waves of five**, two days apart, Tuesday–Thursday, 08:00–10:00 Madrid time.
3. **Never CC two universities on one message.** It is visible, and it tells both of them they
   are one of a list.
4. **Log every send** in the tracker in [`09`](./09-lista-de-envio.md) the moment you press
   send — not later. Forty threads becomes unmanageable in about a week otherwise.
5. **Follow up once**, at ten days, inside the original thread. Never twice, never a new thread.
6. **Stop at four good replies.** Not four replies — four you would be happy to show. Past that
   you are collecting, and the file does not get stronger.

> ⚠️ **Reach is not the goal, and forty threads is not five times better than eight.** The visa
> is earned by the language school's *carta de admisión*, not by this correspondence. A wide
> send is worth doing because it costs nothing and raises the chance of one genuinely good
> reply — the kind that names your degree, gives a date, or opens the equivalence file. Send
> wide, keep four, and put your real effort into the language-school letter and the money.
