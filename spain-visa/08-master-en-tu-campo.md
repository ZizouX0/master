# 08 — The better plan: a master's in software engineering

You meant a master's **in your own field**. That is a stronger plan than the music route on
every axis that matters here, and it fixes two problems the previous files had to work around.

## Why this is the better story

| | Music route | **Software engineering route** |
|---|---|---|
| How the itinerary reads | Engineer → Spanish → music production. An officer *will* wonder | **Engineer → Spanish → engineering master's.** The most ordinary sentence in the file |
| The degree | *Título propio* — not in RUCT, no PhD access | **Máster Universitario Oficial** — ministry-issued, ANECA-verified, EHEA-valid, doctorate-eligible |
| Contact hours | The recurring problem — hybrid, evenings | Engineering masters are **full-time and presencial by default**. Visa *and* prórroga both clear |
| Cost | €4,194 (UCAM) to USD 50,430 (Berklee) | **€2,000–€12,000/year**, and the cheap end is public and excellent |
| Your degree | Something to be excused | **The entry requirement** |

The last row is the real point. In every music programme, your BSc was either irrelevant or a
curiosity. Here it is the thing they are asking for.

> 💡 **And the consular logic gets easier.** The unspoken suspicion behind any language-course
> visa is that the language course *is* the destination. "I am a software engineer, I am
> learning Spanish, then I take an engineering master's taught in Spanish" removes that
> suspicion by being completely unremarkable.

---

## Your foreign degree is not a barrier — and there is a document in it for you

A non-EHEA degree **does not need *homologación*** to enter an official Spanish master's.
The university runs its own **estudio de equivalencia** (a level-equivalence check), for a fee,
and grants access on that basis.

> Access "no implica, en ningún caso, la homologación del título previo" — it lets you study
> the master's, nothing more. That is all you need.

**This is useful beyond admission.** Ask a university to start the equivalence check and you get
a paid, referenced administrative file — a far stronger artefact for your visa dossier than any
email thread. Ask for it in writing:

> *"¿Puedo solicitar el estudio de equivalencia de nivel de mi título tunecino de Ingeniería
> Informática con antelación al plazo de preinscripción, y cuál es la tasa?"*

## The one trap in this tier: online programmes

Spain's cheapest and most welcoming computing masters are **distance-learning**, and a distance
master's **cannot support a study visa** — no presencial contact hours, no permission.

- **UNED**, **UNIR**, **UOC**, **UDIMA** — fully online. Excluded.
- **U-tad** offers five official masters and **three of them are online** (Escultura Digital,
  IA Aplicada a la Ingeniería del Software, Seguridad Informática y Auditoría Forense). Only
  two are presencial.

Check the delivery mode **before** anything else, every time. It is the fastest way to waste a
fortnight in this tier.

### 🇪🇸 B. Spanish-taught public — cheaper, less competitive, and it matches your language year

Universities that publish a **dedicated route for foreign non-homologated degrees**, which
means the process is routine rather than a favour: **Universidad de Sevilla**, **Universidad de
Alicante**, **Universidad de Salamanca**, **Universidad Miguel Hernández**, **Universidad de
Granada**. All run a *Máster Universitario en Ingeniería Informática*.

This tier is the natural fit if you are spending a year getting to B2 anyway — and Sevilla,
Granada and Salamanca are considerably cheaper to live in than Madrid or Barcelona.

## 🎧 And the music plan does not die

The bridge is real, and one programme sits right on it:

**U-tad — *Máster Universitario en Computación Gráfica, Realidad Virtual y Simulación*.**
Official, **presencial**, Spanish-taught, aimed at video games and interactive applications.
Graphics, VR and simulation is the same industry, the same engine work and the same studios as
the game-audio content ENTI teaches — approached from the engineering side, with an official
degree at the end.

A computer-science master's plus your own production portfolio is arguably a **better** route
into game audio or audio tooling than a *título propio* in music production. You would be the
engineer who can also produce, which is a rarer and more employable thing than a producer with
a non-official diploma.

Keep **ENTI–UB** on the list too ([`07`](./07-son-masters-de-verdad.md)) — its intake names
developers, and it is genuinely your subject. Just know it is a título propio, and now you know
what the alternative looks like.

## The timing changes — this matters

Unlike the *título propio* crowd, official masters run on **calendars**, not rolling doors:

- **Public universities close applications around April–May** for the autumn intake, and admit
  by academic record — it is competitive.
- **Private universities admit on a rolling basis**, typically into July.

So for a **September/October 2027** start you are applying in **spring 2027**, from Spain,
mid-language-year. That is comfortable — but it means the equivalence check should be started
**early**, because it is the slow step and it is the one you can do from Tunisia right now.

## The email to send

Same two-question shape as [`05`](./05-correspondencia-universidad.md), retargeted:

**Subject:** `Acceso a máster con título extranjero — Ingeniería Informática (Túnez), curso 2027/28`

```
Estimados señores:

Soy [NOMBRE Y APELLIDOS], ciudadano tunecino y titulado en Ingeniería Informática por
[UNIVERSIDAD] ([AÑO]). Me interesa cursar el [MÁSTER UNIVERSITARIO EN ...] en el curso
2027/28.

Quisiera confirmar dos puntos antes de iniciar el proceso:

1. ¿Puedo acceder al máster con mi título tunecino sin homologación, mediante el estudio
   de equivalencia de nivel de la universidad? ¿Cuál es la tasa y el plazo de resolución?
2. ¿En qué fechas se abre la preinscripción para el curso 2027/28, y cuántas horas
   lectivas semanales presenciales tiene el programa?

Cursaré previamente un año de español presencial en España para alcanzar el nivel
requerido, por lo que conocer el calendario con antelación me resulta necesario.

Muchas gracias por su tiempo.

Un cordial saludo,
[NOMBRE Y APELLIDOS] · [+216 ...] · [correo]
```

Question 2's second half does double duty: it is a natural thing for an applicant to ask, and
the answer is the number your prórroga depends on.
