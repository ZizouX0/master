# 01 — Getting real pre-admission (or a documented exchange) for the master's

**Goal:** a PDF, on letterhead or from an official university address, that names *you*, names
*the master's*, and names *the intake*. Anything in that family works. In descending order of
strength:

| Strength | What it is | How to ask |
|---|---|---|
| ★★★ | **Carta de admisión condicional** — "admitted for 2027/28 subject to X" | Email A, after applying |
| ★★ | **Justificante de preinscripción** — the receipt the portal generates when you register your application | Just apply; download the PDF |
| ★★ | A signed reply from the **international office** confirming your profile is eligible and inviting you to apply for the 2027/28 intake | Email A |
| ★ | A plain email thread with an admissions officer at an `@university.es` address | Any reply at all |

> ⚡ **Only want the exchange, not an application?** Go to [`05`](./05-correspondencia-universidad.md) instead — a two-question email that gets replies, and how to file the replies as evidence. This file is the long form, for when you are really applying.

Even the last one is worth having: it is a documented exchange with the institution, which is
exactly what turns "I hope to do a master's" into "I am already in contact about one".

> 📌 **Timing.** You cannot usually be *admitted* two years early — most 2027/28 portals open
> around Nov 2026–Mar 2027. So ask for what a university *can* give you now: written
> confirmation of eligibility and of the itinerary. Then, once the portal opens while you are
> in Spain, convert it into a real admission letter. Send Email A now; send Email B the week
> applications open.

## Email A — pre-admission enquiry (send now)

**To:** the master's programme coordinator **and** `internacional@` / `admisiones@` of the
university. Send to both; the international office is the one that understands visas.

**Subject:** `Preinscripción y admisión condicional — Máster en [NOMBRE DEL MÁSTER], curso 2027/28 — estudiante tunecino`

```
Estimado/a Sr./Sra.:

Me llamo [NOMBRE Y APELLIDOS], soy ciudadano tunecino y titulado en Ingeniería
Informática por [UNIVERSIDAD, TÚNEZ] (promoción [AÑO]). Mi objetivo profesional es la
producción musical y el diseño sonoro, y su [NOMBRE DEL MÁSTER] es el programa que mejor
encaja con esa trayectoria.

Mi plan formativo tiene dos etapas:

1. Curso 2026/27 — curso intensivo de español de [Nº] horas lectivas semanales,
   presencial, en [CIUDAD], hasta alcanzar el nivel [B2/C1] acreditado por DELE o SIELE.
2. Curso 2027/28 — incorporación a su máster.

Para poder tramitar el visado de estudios ante la Embajada de España en Túnez necesito
acreditar la coherencia de este itinerario. Por ello les agradecería que me indicaran:

1. Si mi titulación de Ingeniería Informática cumple el perfil de acceso del máster.
2. El nivel de español exigido para la admisión y si se acredita con DELE o SIELE.
3. Las fechas de apertura del plazo de preinscripción para el curso 2027/28.
4. Si la universidad puede emitirme un documento de preinscripción o una carta de
   admisión condicional para el curso 2027/28, indicando el programa y el curso académico.
5. El número de horas lectivas presenciales semanales del máster y si la docencia es
   íntegramente presencial. (Este dato es determinante para la prórroga de mi estancia
   por estudios, que exige un mínimo de 20 horas lectivas semanales presenciales.)

Adjunto mi CV y mi expediente académico. Quedo a su disposición para una entrevista en
línea o para enviar cualquier documentación adicional.

Agradeciéndoles de antemano su tiempo, reciban un cordial saludo,

[NOMBRE Y APELLIDOS]
[Teléfono con +216] · [correo electrónico]
[Ciudad], Túnez
```

**Attach:** CV (2 pages), degree certificate + transcript (Spanish or English translation),
and — if you have one — a link to a portfolio of your work. For a music programme a portfolio
link does more than another paragraph of text.

### English version (for Berklee Valencia and English-taught programmes)

**Subject:** `Conditional admission enquiry — [PROGRAMME NAME], 2027/28 intake — applicant from Tunisia`

```
Dear Admissions Team,

My name is [FULL NAME]. I am a Tunisian national and a graduate in Software Engineering
from [UNIVERSITY, TUNISIA] ([YEAR]). I am planning my path into music production and sound
design, and [PROGRAMME NAME] is my target programme.

My plan has two stages: an intensive, in-person Spanish language year in [CITY] during
2026/27, and then entry to your master's for the 2027/28 intake.

To support my Spanish study-visa application I need to evidence that this itinerary is
coherent, so I would be grateful if you could confirm:

1. Whether my Software Engineering degree meets the entry profile.
2. The English (and Spanish, if relevant) level required, and accepted certificates.
3. When applications for the 2027/28 intake open.
4. Whether you can issue a pre-admission or conditional admission letter naming the
   programme and academic year.
5. The number of scheduled in-person contact hours per week, and whether teaching is fully
   on campus — this determines whether the programme supports a Spanish study permit,
   which requires at least 20 in-person hours per week.

My CV and transcript are attached, along with a link to my portfolio. I am happy to
interview online or send any further documentation.

Kind regards,
[FULL NAME]
[+216 phone] · [email]
[City], Tunisia
```

## Email B — converting it into an admission letter (send when the portal opens)

**Subject:** `Solicitud de carta de admisión — [NOMBRE], solicitud nº [Nº EXPEDIENTE], Máster en [X] 2027/28`

```
Estimado/a Sr./Sra.:

He formalizado mi preinscripción en el Máster en [X] para el curso 2027/28 (número de
solicitud [Nº], fecha [FECHA]).

Al ser ciudadano no comunitario, necesito presentar ante las autoridades españolas una
carta de admisión que incluya expresamente:

- mis datos personales y número de pasaporte;
- la denominación oficial del programa y el curso académico;
- las fechas de inicio y fin del programa;
- el número de horas lectivas semanales y su carácter presencial;
- el importe de la matrícula y el estado del pago;
- firma y sello de la universidad.

Estos datos son los que exige la normativa de extranjería, y una carta que omita las horas
lectivas semanales suele derivar en un requerimiento de subsanación. Les agradecería que la
carta los incluyera todos.

Quedo a su disposición para cualquier trámite o pago necesario.

Un cordial saludo,
[NOMBRE Y APELLIDOS]
```

From [`results/PRIVATE-SCHOOLS.md`](../results/PRIVATE-SCHOOLS.md):

- **UCAM Murcia** — Máster en Producción Musical, €3,900, your degree is named in its entry

- **La Salle – URL, Barcelona** — real university, but the timetable is 18:00–20:00. Use it as
  correspondence evidence if you like; do not name it as the itinerary's destination.
- **Berklee Valencia** — full-time, presencial, visa-safe, ≈ USD 50,430. Early Action opens
  around Nov 2026.

And do not restrict yourself to Spain for the *master's* half — the language visa is Spanish,
the master's does not have to be. A pre-admission from anywhere in the EU still evidences the
itinerary; see [`results/ARTIST-ROUTE.md`](../results/ARTIST-ROUTE.md).

## If nobody replies

Normal — international offices are slow and two years is early. Escalate in this order:
the programme coordinator's direct address → the international/mobility office → the
university's phone number, in Spanish, at 10:00–13:00 Madrid time. **Send from one email
address and keep every reply as PDF**, thread intact, sender address visible. A printed
thread is evidence; a screenshot of a chat is not.
